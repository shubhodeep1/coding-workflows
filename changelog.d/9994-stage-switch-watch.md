- **Daily report of pipeline stage switches left off.** Four steps at the end of the daily `collect-logs` job in `workflow-log-analysis.yml` read the repository variables once (`GH_PAT`, one paginated listing), check every on/off switch in the new registry `scripts/stage_switches.json` (every `vars.<NAME>_ENABLED || 'true'` switch, 168 today), and send one Telegram WARNING per switch that has been off longer than `STAGE_SWITCH_OFF_ALERT_HOURS` (default 24), at most once per UTC day. A switch can be marked off on purpose, with an expiry date, in the registry's `off_on_purpose` list. `STAGE_SWITCH_WATCH_ENABLED` (default `true`) turns the watch off. A failed read only warns. `tests/test_stage_switch_registry.py` fails when a workflow or the README adds a default-on switch the registry does not list. Previously `FINAL_CI_FAILED_REDISPATCH_ENABLED=false` silently kept failed final CI from reaching autofix for days. Refs #9994, Refs #9897, Refs #9956.
