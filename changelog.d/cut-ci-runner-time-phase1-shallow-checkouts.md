- **Three slow CI jobs no longer clone full history.** `tests-hooks-and-orchestrator`, `tests-release-and-log-analysis` and the 24-shard `tests-system-python` matrix in `.github/workflows/ci.yml` now check out at depth 1. A new step in each job ("Fetch base commits for the shallow checkout (...)") then fetches only what the history readers need: a 200-commit window of HEAD and `main`, plus the pull-request base or push `before` commit. It unshallows those two refs only when the merge-base is deeper. Pushes to `main` and `stable` fetch nothing, because nothing reads a base there. A pull request whose base or merge-base cannot be fetched fails the job with an `::error::` line, instead of the parity and drift tests running without history. A branch-creation or force push keeps its existing fallback to the merge-base with `main`, with a warning. The optional `SHALLOW_FETCH_RETRY_DELAY_SECONDS` (default 5) sets the backoff between the three fetch attempts. `tests/test_ci_shallow_checkout_contract.py` pins the layout and runs the step against a local origin. Refs #9528.
