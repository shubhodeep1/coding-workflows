- **Review autofix logs free disk space before and after cleanup (#8725).** The `codex-agent` job of `.github/workflows/review_autofix.yml` gains a `Show disk usage (pre free-disk-space)` step right before `Free disk space`, and both it and the existing post step log exact byte counts with `df -B1 --output=avail,size /`. The headroom the cleanup reclaims can now be computed from the job log before anyone considers skipping it. The cleanup's duration is the `Free disk space` step's own Actions step timing. Logging only: the pre step never fails the job, and the cleanup step, its pinned action and its settings are unchanged. `tests/test_review_autofix_disk_headroom_contract.py` pins the step order and commands.
