- **A plan's `Scope-mode` justification no longer counts as files the plan changes.** A plan for consumer `tele-funtoken-msg-scoring` #5101 put its `Scope-mode:`, `Reuse-audit:` and `Scope-mode justification:` lines right under the `## Files likely to change` bullets, with no heading in between. The justification named three files the plan had chosen not to change, and the plan approval gate held it with `plan_files_outside_files_touched`, so the issue waited for a maintainer's `/approved`.

  `extract_plan_file_paths` in `scripts/files_touched_scope_guard.py` now skips each `Scope-mode:`, `Scope-mode justification:`, `Reuse-audit:` or `Implementation-time estimate:` line (bold or not, as a plain line or a list item at any indent, in any letter case) together with its prose continuation, and resumes at the next blank line or file bullet. Files listed before or after these lines are still compared with `files_touched`, the pipeline plan grant and the heal scope; only the paths named in the metadata prose are left out. The protected-path scan (`.github/`, `.claude/`, `scripts/`, `prompts/`, `workflow-templates/`) still reads the whole section, so a protected token in the justification still holds the plan. The plan prompt (`prompts/_templates/mode-plan.txt`, `prompts/mode-plan.txt` and the inline fallback in `scripts/run_plan_codex.sh`) now tells the planner to put those lines after Testing considerations.

  What this means for operators: plans that explain rejected out-of-scope fixes in their Scope-mode block are auto-approved again when their file list fits `files_touched`. The implement-time `files_touched` guard is unchanged and still refuses any commit outside the list.
