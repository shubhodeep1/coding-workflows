<!-- changelog: changed -->
- **Long-running, protection-required check-runs now have a shorter review-context wait.** When every independently pending check is confirmed required and has been running for at least five minutes, the collector caps its wait at 60 seconds. Unknown, queued, recently started or unverifiable checks retain the existing 300-second default; a slow protection lookup is bounded by the remaining configured wait. A timed-out snapshot still shows pending checks, and the final merge gate remains unchanged.

The September 3 workflow-analysis changes also skip reviewer pass two only after every active reviewer reports a complete clean pass and local head, path, diff, and merge evidence remains safe; uncertain evidence still runs the second pass. `AI_MEMORY_TELEMETRY` adds `elapsed_ms` and attempted-stage `clone_ms`, `fetch_ms`, `rebase_ms`, `commit_ms`, `push_ms`, and `retry_sleep_ms`. `SEMBLE_QUERY` adds `query_bytes`, `returned_bytes`, and `included_bytes`, while `deduplicated_bytes` and `avoided_prompt_bytes` explicitly report `unavailable`; optional Semble indexing and queries now run in the credential-free container sandbox. Operators can measure review decisions, memory writes, and Semble context without inferring unobserved work.
