- **claude-pool-broker can now hold ChatGPT account logins, and a new `chatgpt-pool-token` action hands one to a job.** The broker gains a `ChatgptAccount` Durable Object store with `POST /v1/chatgpt` (the least-used account under the 0.9 gate, refresh token removed, or `503 chatgpt_pool_empty`), `GET /v1/chatgpt/status` (metadata only) and writer-only `GET` / `PUT /v1/chatgpt/refresh/<NAME>` for claude-workers' `chatgpt-pool-refresh.yml`, with a generation compare-and-swap. `.github/actions/chatgpt-pool-token` writes the login to `$RUNNER_TEMP/chatgpt-pool/<NAME>/auth.json` (mode `token`) or reports the accounts' status (mode `status`), never fails the job and deletes the files at the end. Nothing calls it yet, and `/v1/pool` is unchanged. Refs #8969
