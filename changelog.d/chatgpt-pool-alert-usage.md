- **ChatGPT account health in the hourly Claude pool alert and the daily usage report.** `orchestrate_poll.yml` gains a "Read ChatGPT pool status" step (`.github/actions/chatgpt-pool-token`, `mode: status`, metadata only, never a token) in the `poll` job and in the `claude_pool_daily_usage` job. `claude_engine.py pool-health` / `pool-usage` accept `--chatgpt-status` (plus `--chatgpt-stale-hours`, and `--chatgpt-reason` for `pool-usage`). The hourly near-cap message (`scripts/claude_pool_health_alert.sh`) adds a `ChatGPT pool:` block when an account's login was rejected, its last refresh failed, its last refresh is at least `CHATGPT_POOL_ALERT_STALE_HOURS` (default `12`) old or missing (all `WARNING`), or its usage is at or above the broker gate or its quota is exhausted (`DEBUG`). It goes out in the same hourly window as the Claude part, and `CHATGPT_POOL_HEALTH_ALERT_ENABLED=false` drops it. The daily `📊` report (`scripts/claude_pool_daily_usage.sh send`) appends a "ChatGPT accounts" section, or `ChatGPT accounts: unavailable (<reason>).` when the status could not be read; with no ChatGPT accounts both messages are unchanged. The `CLAUDE_POOL_HEALTH` and `CLAUDE_POOL_DAILY_USAGE op=send` log lines gain `chatgpt_accounts=N chatgpt_alerts=N` at the end. Phase 4 of the ChatGPT account reviewer trial plan; Refs #8969.
