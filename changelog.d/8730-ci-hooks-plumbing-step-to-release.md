- **A test step moved out of the CI job `tests-hooks-and-orchestrator`.** That job took 15m07s of its 20-minute timeout on `main` (run 41570422282, budget-watch issue #8730). The step `Review autofix review-pipeline plumbing contract test` now runs unchanged, under the same name, once per CI run, at the end of `tests-release-and-log-analysis`. It was the hooks job's largest step not split into parallel processes (9 modules, about 367 test functions) and arrived there in #8395. `tests-release-and-log-analysis` was picked because it has had no budget-watch issue of its own since #8278, the same basis #8466 used, and it already has the checkout, Python 3.12 and pip install the step needs. The run's step timings could not be read, so the step was picked by estimated cost (test and subprocess counts), not measured, and the release job's remaining headroom is likewise an estimate. No job was added or renamed, no `timeout-minutes` changed, and `CI / lint` is unchanged; operators have nothing to do. If either job comes near its timeout, the next `budget-watch` run on `main` opens an issue for it that links the run's step timings. `tests/test_ci_poll_test_sharding.py` now checks that the moved step runs once, unguarded, in its new job.
