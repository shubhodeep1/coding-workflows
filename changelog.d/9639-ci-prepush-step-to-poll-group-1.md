A job that reaches its timeout turns CI red even when the code is fine. This step was group 3's newest load (#9391 added it, and group 3 was not flagged after #9037 until then) and likely the heavier of its two pinned steps: about 472 tests in 10 serial pytest runs. It is now pinned to group 0 (`if: strategy.job-index == 0`). The target was picked by how much its pinned steps weigh, not how many there are. Group 0 pins the fast-fail subset (11 tests) and the triage step (about 162s, #9450). Group 1 was avoided: it pins the review-pipeline plumbing step (about 367 tests and 145 subprocess sites), which #8805 kept apart from another heavy step after group 1 took 16m44s. Group 0 has the same checkout, Python 3.12 and pip set (ruff included). The run's step results gave statuses only, with no durations, so the pick is an estimate from test and subprocess counts, not a measurement. The step runs unchanged, under the same name, once per CI run. No job was added or renamed, no `timeout-minutes` changed, the matrix size is unchanged and `CI / lint` is unchanged; operators have nothing to do. If `orchestrate-poll (0)` comes near its timeout next, measure the step timings before moving anything again. `MovedPollGroupThree9639StepsContractTest` in `tests/test_ci_poll_test_sharding.py` checks that the step runs once, in group 0 only, has left group 3, avoids the last group and the plumbing step's group, runs after the poll job's setup and still names its ruff check and every module.
