- **The CI size watcher now warns before the 430,000-byte contract-test ceiling, and `review_autofix.yml` has 47 KB of headroom again.**

`review_autofix.yml` had grown to 429,400 bytes, 600 bytes under the 430,000-byte ceiling three contract tests enforce, while the `budget-watch` job only warned at 440,000 bytes. Nine open PRs went over the ceiling once merged with `main` and learned it from a red final CI. Four more `codex-agent` step bodies ("Telegram review-blocked judge decision", "Push all pending commits", "Pre-assemble static context" and "Resolve AI engine") moved to `scripts/review_autofix_step_*.sh` with their steps' `name:`, `id:`, `if:` and `env:` kept. The #10208 fallback checks then added about 19 KB to the workflow, so two more bodies moved: "Dispatch standalone validate for orchestrator short-circuit issues" (job `post-merge-validate-dispatch`) and "Apply identical-failure cap outcome" (job `fingerprint-cap-block`). Those two jobs have no staged support bundle, so their steps source the script only from the job's own `.codex-workflow-src` checkout, after "Verify workflow support source" checked it and only while its HEAD still equals `WORKFLOW_SUPPORT_REF`; otherwise the step fails. The workflow is now 383,056 bytes on this pull request's head, 46,944 bytes under the 430,000-byte test ceiling; the issue's 380,000-byte target is not yet met and is left to a follow-up. "Capture runtime context" stays inline because it runs under `always()`, possibly before the trusted support checkout, and the wrapper's `.codex-workflow-src` fallback would then source a file from the PR tree. The "Mark linked issues review-blocked (autofix exhaustion)" step keeps sourcing #9654's `scripts/review_autofix_step_mark_review_blocked_exhaustion.sh`, and the "Telegram editor-noop-suspicious warning" step keeps sourcing `scripts/review_autofix_step_editor_noop_suspicious_warning.sh` from #9944. The Telegram steps read `github.server_url`, `github.repository` and `github.run_id` from `AUTOFIX_STEP_SERVER_URL`, `AUTOFIX_STEP_REPOSITORY` and `AUTOFIX_STEP_RUN_ID` in their `env:`.

`scripts/ci_budget_watch.py` gains `WORKFLOW_TEST_CEILING_BYTES` (430,000), which the three size tests now import, and the `CI_BUDGET_WORKFLOW_WARN_BYTES` default drops from 440,000 to 400,000, in the watcher and in the `budget-watch` step's fallback in `ci.yml`. A configured value at or above the test ceiling is kept but logs a warning. Workflow-size findings are no longer held back by the re-file cooldown. Expected first effect: `test-and-mark-stable.yml` (407,289 bytes) and `implement.yml` (404,457 bytes) get `ai:ci-budget` issues on the next push to `main`.
