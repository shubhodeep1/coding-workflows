- **A PR whose merge-lease hold ran out while its runs waited for a runner is granted the lease again once its review passes.** Since #9983 a released holder's skip entry kept it out of the lease until its head changed, unless the reason was `revoked_no_head_gate`. A holder released as `expired` or `revoked_idle` is usually healthy, so with no new push it was never granted again (#9897 stayed parked on `2718eb27` after a 4 h 19 min hold). Skip entries with reason `expired` or `revoked_idle` now stop blocking once the PR's pipeline `ai-review/head-gate` is `success` on that head, and the PR takes its normal queue place. The tick logs `MERGE_LEASE ... outcome=unparked reason=head_gate_passed_after_<expired|revoked_idle>`. To stop one PR cycling through the lease, this happens at most once per head: the grant keeps the entry as `expired_unparked` / `revoked_idle_unparked`, and a second `expired` or `revoked_idle` release of the same head stays parked until the head changes. A head-gate failure in between does not reset this: its `revoked_no_head_gate_unparked` entry still clears once the head-gate passes, and the grant keeps the marker. `revoked_draft`, `revoked_skip_ai`, `revoked_final_ci_failed` and other reasons are unchanged. No new variable, record field or API call; `MERGE_LEASE_REVOKE_ENABLED=false` keeps the old behaviour. Operators have nothing to do.

Refs #10044, Refs #9983, Refs #9897.
