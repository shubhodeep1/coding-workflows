- **The daily workflow updater no longer fails with a false `ERR_SYNC_MANIFEST_MISSING` when the shared `GH_PAT` is rate limited.** The 04:00 UTC updater run overlaps the poller, and the manifest download was its first API call: a rate-limit 403 was hidden and reported as a missing manifest. "Verify signed sync manifest" (in `update_workflows.yml` and `workflow_integrity.yml`) now looks the release up and downloads `sync-manifest.json` over REST, waits for the rate-limit reset or the `Retry-After` the response gives, up to 20 minutes in total (repository variable `SYNC_MANIFEST_RATE_LIMIT_MAX_WAIT_SECONDS`, default `1200`, at most `1500`), retries other errors once, and logs each attempt as `SYNC_MANIFEST_FETCH`. Fetch failures that remain are reported as the new `ERR_SYNC_MANIFEST_FETCH_FAILED` (`detail=rate_limited|http_<status>|unknown`); `ERR_SYNC_MANIFEST_MISSING` now means the release or its asset really is absent.

What this means for consumer repos: after the next `@stable` sync, the updater and the wrapper integrity check survive an exhausted `GH_PAT` budget instead of failing, and both of their job timeouts rise to 35 minutes.
