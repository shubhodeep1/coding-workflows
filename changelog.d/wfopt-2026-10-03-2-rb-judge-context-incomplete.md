- **The review-blocked judge no longer merges a PR whose comments it could not fully read.** When the PR comment context in `scripts/review_rb_judge.sh` is incomplete (the comments helper reports `"comments_incomplete": true`, or a paginated comments read in the inline legacy branch fails), the judge logs `RB_JUDGE_CONTEXT_INCOMPLETE`, tells the judge prompt that comments may be missing, posts an approval as a plain comment review, and withholds every merge-enabling outcome for that run: auto-merge, direct merge, the `ai:ready-to-merge` label and the head-gate success status. It writes `judge_action=skip` / `judge_skip_reason=comments_context_incomplete` and leaves the PR for the next judge cycle. Non-merge actions and runs with a complete context are unchanged. Refs #8895.
