- **The unblock judge no longer holds an item forever on an operator step nobody can finish by setting a variable.** When an operator-step fix-up merges, the judge holds the item (`hold=placeholder_retired`) only when the step's recorded instructions tell a person to set a repository variable or secret with exactly the placeholder's name, the one condition it can check. Any other step (pinning an action SHA, granting workflow paths) is now escalated to a person once, with the recorded instructions, and the item stays parked until someone posts its resume command. A model `wait` on such a placeholder is refused. Holds that are already open are left alone and expire as before. Refs #9484, shubhodeep1/mongo-explorer#51, shubhodeep1/radateeree-resort.com#174.
