- **The review "Checkout PR head branch" step reports real fetch and total timings again on ubuntu-26.04 runners.** `review_head_checkout_now_ms` in `scripts/review_autofix_step_checkout_pr_head_branch.sh` now reads bash's `EPOCHREALTIME` first. On the new image `date +%s%3N` sometimes printed a reading one digit short, so the fetch start and the exit-trap clock came from different fallbacks, the elapsed time went negative and the `REVIEW_HEAD_CHECKOUT` line printed `fetch_ms=na`. `date` stays as the fallback for bash < 5, and the whole-seconds fallback now forces base 10. The line format, retry policy, exit codes and `$GITHUB_ENV` outputs are unchanged. A regression test in `tests/test_review_checkout_head_diagnostics.py` feeds a short `date` reading and checks the helper ignores it. Refs #9405.
