- **The CI budget watch no longer reports jobs that never ran.** `scripts/ci_budget_watch.py` now measures a job's duration from its first started step to its completion, and skips a job that ran no step.

Run 43771105041 was cancelled while its test jobs were still waiting for runners. None of them ran a step, but the `budget-watch` job measured each one from the job's `started_at` to its `completed_at` and reported `tests-release-and-log-analysis` as taking 33m49s of its 20-minute `timeout-minutes`, which opened a bogus `ai:ci-budget` issue (Refs #9559). The wait for a runner no longer counts, and a job that ran no step is logged with a `::notice::ci_budget_watch:` line instead of a finding. A job that really hits its timeout ran its steps, so it is still reported even though GitHub marks it `cancelled`. When the jobs response has no `steps` list, the job's own `started_at` is used as before. Flags, environment variables, issue text and the dedup marker are unchanged.
