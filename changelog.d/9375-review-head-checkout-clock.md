- **A regression test now guards the review step's `REVIEW_HEAD_CHECKOUT` timing against a `date` clock that goes backwards.** On ubuntu-26.04 runners `date +%s%3N` gave readings that went backwards within a run, which would make `fetch_ms` and `switch_ms` come out as `na`. The clock order itself (`EPOCHREALTIME`, then `date +%s%3N`, then `date +%s`) already shipped with #9252 (`changelog.d/9252-epochrealtime-ms-clock.md`). This change adds `test_elapsed_fields_ignore_a_date_clock_that_goes_backwards` to `tests/test_review_checkout_head_diagnostics.py`, plus a comment in `review_head_checkout_now_ms` (`scripts/review_autofix_step_checkout_pr_head_branch.sh`) explaining why bash's clock comes first. Runtime behaviour and the line format are unchanged.
