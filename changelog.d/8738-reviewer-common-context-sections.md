- **Full and scoped reviewer prompts now share one copy of their untrusted-input sections.** In `scripts/review_run_reviewers.sh`, the PR comments (cap 150000), check-runs (cap 80000) and slop-scan findings (cap 40000) `UNTRUSTED` sections are emitted by a new `emit_reviewer_common_context_sections()` helper. `emit_full_reviewer_prompt_context_sections()` and `emit_scoped_reviewer_prompt_context_sections()` both call it, so the trust-boundary wording can no longer drift between full and scoped review. The rendered prompt text is byte-identical, and the new `tests/test_review_reviewer_common_context_sections.py` pins both builders' output and checks that each header exists only once in the script. Refs #8723.
