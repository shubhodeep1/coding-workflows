- **The required-checks wait reads less and backs off while nothing changes.** `_pr_wait_for_required_checks` in `scripts/pr_checks_lib.sh` now reads branch protection once per wait instead of on every poll, and reads the head's commit statuses (gate-supersede path) once per head SHA unless one is still `pending`. The poll interval starts at `AUTO_MERGE_CHECKS_POLL_SECONDS` and doubles after each poll whose snapshot is unchanged, up to the new `AUTO_MERGE_CHECKS_POLL_MAX_SECONDS` (default `180`); a change resets it, and the last sleep is clamped to the `AUTO_MERGE_CHECKS_WAIT_MINUTES` deadline.

The wait was the biggest `GH_PAT` read loop in a review run: four reads every 60 s for up to 45 minutes. When `gh_read` (`scripts/gh_helpers.sh`) is defined, the per-poll reads (runs listing, check-runs, statuses, PR read) of a wait with a budget go through `gh_read --same-repo-only` with the new `GH_READ_TOKEN` env (the job token) on the `Enable auto-merge on PR` step, the `deterministic-skip-merge` job and the review-blocked judge step. Branch protection, the identity read and every write stay on the PAT. The "no check-runs registered yet" grace still takes two base-interval polls, a 0-minute wait (merge train, poller) makes the same calls as before, and the `AUTOFIX_AUTO_MERGE_CHECKS ... outcome=` values are unchanged. Setting `AUTO_MERGE_CHECKS_POLL_MAX_SECONDS` to the base interval turns the back-off off. Refs #9926.
