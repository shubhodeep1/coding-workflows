- **Items stopped by a GH_PAT rate limit are now picked up again.** When the primary rate limit stopped clarify's "Resolve integration ref" step (resolver rc=75) or the review gate's PR read (`AUTOFIX_GATE_PR_READ_FAILED rc=75`), the run failed and nothing re-entered the issue or PR after the limit reset; items sat idle for about two days until someone resumed them by hand. Both failures now leave a marker comment, posted with the job token, that records the reset time. Clarify also adds `ai:reclarify-requeue`. The orchestrator poller re-enters clarify, or re-dispatches the review, once the reset has passed. It does this at most 3 times per item (per head for PRs), then adds `ai:needs-human` with a comment that names the cause. The poller reuses reads it already makes, and replays only for a trusted source comment or issue author. Consumers receive the change at the next `@stable` sync; nothing to configure. Refs #10031.
