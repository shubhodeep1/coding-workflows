- **ChatGPT account store in claude-pool-broker and the `chatgpt-pool-token` action (not wired yet).** The broker gains a `ChatgptAccount` Durable Object store with `POST /v1/chatgpt` (the usable account with the lowest weekly usage, refresh token removed, or `503 chatgpt_pool_empty`), `GET /v1/chatgpt/status` (metadata only) and writer-only `GET` / `PUT /v1/chatgpt/refresh/<NAME>` with a compare-and-swap on `generation` for `claude-workers`' `chatgpt-pool-refresh.yml`; `/v1/pool` is unchanged and the broker never refreshes a token. The new `.github/actions/chatgpt-pool-token` (`scripts/chatgpt_pool_token.sh`) writes one account's `auth.json` (mode `token`) or the pool status (mode `status`) and never fails the job. Phase 1 of the ChatGPT account reviewer trial plan; Refs #8969.
