- **The orchestrator poller now reports a failed promote-cycle promotion to the workflow heal intake (stuck-states P4, Refs #8291).** When a verifying run's promotion ends in failure, `scripts/orchestrate_poll_process.sh` sends one `release_blocker` heal report: `promotion_failed:<reason>` for `release_workflow_not_pinnable`, `dispatch_failed`, `release_failed`, `hold_timeout`, or a pre-dispatch check still unavailable at the hold cap (`pin_check_unavailable`, `quiescence_check_unavailable`, `stable_branch_compare_unavailable`, `stable_tag_lookup_unavailable`). It also reports `verification_not_dispatched` when a proving run's verifying run could not be dispatched within `COMPREHENSIVE_VERIFICATION_RETRY_MAX` retries, and `marker_untrusted` on the first alert about an untrusted apply-analysis marker. Only this repository's `internal-orchestrate-poll.yml` reports, so consumer pollers send nothing. A `release_blocker_reported` flag in the tracking issue's state stops the same outcome being reported twice. Reports log `RELEASE_BLOCKER_REPORTED`; a report that could not be built or sent logs `RELEASE_BLOCKER_REPORT_SKIPPED` and sends a Telegram WARNING. Existing comments, alerts and merge decisions do not change. Kill switch: `RELEASE_BLOCKER_HEAL_ENABLED=false`.
