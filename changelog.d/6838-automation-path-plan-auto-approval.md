- Add `AUTOMATION_PATH_PLAN_AUTO_APPROVAL_ENABLED` (default `false`) to hold the automatic `/approved` for plans that list automation paths (`.github/`, `.claude/`, `scripts/`, `prompts/`, `workflow-templates/`) without an exact `files_touched` grant from a trusted author in the issue body. Plans without automation paths still auto-approve, and a human `/approved` and the implement commit-time guard are unchanged. With the flag off, auto-approval works exactly as before.
