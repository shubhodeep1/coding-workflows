- **The unblock judge now closes a "nothing to implement" issue even when no fix-up wait is pending.** A standalone `ai:blocked` issue whose newest trusted "Planning blocked" comment reads `Reason: work is already on the planning ref; nothing to implement` is closed as completed. Before, this only happened right after a fix-up wait ended.

#9215 was blocked with that reason at 07:46Z, after the wait for its fix-up PR #9246 had already ended and posted `/answer` at 03:49Z. Until now the judge closed such issues only inside the fix-up-wait-done branch (#8470). The issue stayed open with `ai:blocked`, raised a CRITICAL alert for work that was already done, and had to be closed by hand. `scripts/unblock_judge.sh` now also calls `unblock_nothing_to_implement_close` on the path with no pending wait (no wait at all, or a wait already marked `done`). This happens after the deterministic exits and before the model judge. The safety checks are the same: the newest block comes from the pipeline login, no trusted implementation plan comes after it, and the issue is standalone with no tracking issue. In addition, `ai:check-triage` issues keep the #9659 gate, and `ai:orchestrator-managed` issues are never closed this way. The comment names no fix-up. Its marker is `<!-- ai:unblock-nothing-to-implement:v1 item=<n> fixup=none -->`, and the log line is `UNBLOCK_JUDGE ... fixup=none op=nothing_to_implement outcome=closed|skip reason=...`. The fix-up path's comment, marker and log lines are unchanged.

What this means for consumer repos: after the next `@stable` sync, standalone issues that planning stopped with "nothing to implement" are closed as completed. They no longer wait for a human or a CRITICAL page. Reopen an issue and reply `/reclarify` if work is still missing. To turn off both the new path and the #8470 fix-up path, set the repository variable `UNBLOCK_NOTHING_TO_IMPLEMENT_CLOSE_ENABLED=false` (default `true`).
