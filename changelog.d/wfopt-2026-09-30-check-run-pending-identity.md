- **The review step's check-run wait now names the checks it is waiting for.** While `scripts/collect_pr_check_runs_context.py` waits for in-flight check-runs, each poll prints one `CHECK_RUNS_WAIT_PENDING` line with the poll number, the in-flight count, how many were dropped as the review's own job (`self_excluded`), and up to 20 `name|app_slug|status|elapsed_s` entries (`truncated=<k>` for the rest). When the wait ends it prints one `CHECK_RUNS_WAIT_EXIT` line with `status=ready|timeout|api_error`, the poll count, seconds waited and the last in-flight count. Check names and app slugs are sanitised so they cannot inject workflow commands, and URLs and summaries are never logged. Wait timeout, poll and backoff intervals, the self-run exclusion and the snapshot file are unchanged, and the existing `Waiting for` and `CHECK_RUNS_WAIT_TIMEOUT` lines are byte-identical. Refs #8724.
