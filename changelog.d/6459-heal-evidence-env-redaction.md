- **Workflow-heal evidence no longer carries step environment values or `Authorization: Basic` credentials.** The sliced job logs that heal agents read keep each step's env variable names but replace their values with `[value omitted]`.

A security audit found that the sliced logs in the heal evidence folder copied the env block of the failing step verbatim, and that their credential redaction caught `Authorization: Bearer` but not `Authorization: Basic <base64>`. Those logs reach the clarify sandbox at `/evidence` and the heal intake's diagnosis prompt, so a credential in either place could be repeated in an issue comment. `scripts/workflow_failure_heal_evidence.py` now reduces every line after `env:` in a step header up to its `##[endgroup]`, including unparsed continuation lines and env blocks cut off by truncation. The one exception is `GIT_DIR` and `GIT_WORK_TREE` when the value is a plain absolute path, because #6055's cause was visible only there. Redaction now also covers Basic credentials (including the `actions/checkout` extraheader form and `Proxy-Authorization`) and replaces the rest of the line for any other authorization scheme.

What this means for operators: nothing to configure. Evidence folders collected before this change are fetched and sliced again on the next stage, and a stale job log is deleted even when the re-fetch fails. The failure fingerprint still comes from `filter_log`, which is unchanged, so open heal lineages continue.
