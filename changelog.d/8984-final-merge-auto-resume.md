- **Final-merge-exhausted projects resume on their own once the final PR goes green (`FINAL_MERGE_AUTO_RESUME_ENABLED`, default on).** A project whose final integration merge failed `MAX_FINAL_MERGE_ATTEMPTS` times used to stay parked (`Project already failed, skipping.`) even after the cause cleared, until a person merged the final PR by hand (fun-token-multi-chain#579 / #596). The exhaustion path now records `final_merge_failure_kind=final_merge_exhausted` plus the final PR head, and each tick checks such a project: when the final PR is open, not a draft, `mergeable` with `mergeable_state=clean`, its required checks are complete and passing, its head is the head runtime validation passed on, and the integration branch is still ahead of the default branch, the poller restores `validating` / `ai:validated`, removes `ai:blocked` and `ai:needs-human`, posts one tracking comment and retries the merge through the normal final-merge gates. It fires at most once per final-PR head SHA, fails closed on any lookup error, and logs `FINAL_MERGE_AUTO_RESUME`. Projects parked before this change, and every other `failed` state, are untouched. Set the repo variable to `false` to keep today's behaviour.
