- **A project parked after its final merge ran out of attempts now resumes on its own once the blocker clears.** The poller retries the final merge once per final PR head SHA when that PR is open, not a draft, mergeable with `mergeable_state=clean`, its required checks are green, its head is the SHA that passed validation, and the integration branch is still ahead of the default branch.

Before, after `MAX_FINAL_MERGE_ATTEMPTS` failed merges a project stayed `failed` with `ai:blocked` and every tick logged `Project already failed, skipping.`, even after the final PR went green (shubhodeep1/fun-token-multi-chain#579: final PR #596 was green and clean four days before a person merged it by hand). The exhaustion path now records `final_merge_failure_kind=final_merge_exhausted` and `final_merge_failure_head_sha`. The retry restores `ai:validated` (dropping `ai:blocked` and `ai:needs-human`), resets `final_merge_attempt_count`, posts one tracking comment and runs through `mark_validation_complete`, so the security pass, required-checks gate and head-bound merge still apply. If the retry fails the project parks again until the PR's head changes. Every decision is logged as `FINAL_MERGE_AUTO_RESUME`. Set `FINAL_MERGE_AUTO_RESUME_ENABLED=false` to keep the old behaviour. Projects that failed for any other reason, or that parked before this change, are left alone.
