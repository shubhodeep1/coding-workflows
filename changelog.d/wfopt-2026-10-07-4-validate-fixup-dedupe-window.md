- **The validation fix-up dedupe lookup now covers up to 1000 open managed issues and reports why it missed (BUG-003).** In the `needs_fixes` path of `scripts/validate_process.sh`, the per-tracker dedupe `gh issue list` bound rises from 200 to 1000 (held in `FIXUP_DEDUPE_LIST_LIMIT`), so an older open fix-up for the same tracker and cycle is less likely to fall outside the window and be duplicated. A failed lookup, an unparsable response, and a no-match result that filled the whole window now each emit a distinct `::warning::` line carrying only the tracker, cycle, count and limit. Fix-up creation stays fail-open, and the reuse and create paths are otherwise unchanged. Refs #9089.
