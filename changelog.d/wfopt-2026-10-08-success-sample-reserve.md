- **Workflow log collection now samples successful runs even when notable runs fill the cap.** `select_notable_runs_for_logs()` in `scripts/collect_workflow_logs.py` reserves up to `max(1, floor(max_log_runs * SUCCESS_SAMPLE_MAX_RESERVE_FRACTION))` slots (10%, one slot at the default `--max-log-runs 15`) for deterministically sampled successful runs that sit outside the failed, retried and slow buckets, bounded by `ceil(candidates * success_sample_rate)`. Failed runs still lead, reserve slots the sample cannot use go back to notable runs, and the reserve is skipped when `--success-sample-rate` is 0 or `--max-log-runs` is 1. Previously the report showed 124/1,000 runs with parsed telemetry, a 7% configured sample and 0 sampled successes. The categorized log export is unchanged because its categories are capped independently (workflow-optimization-2026-10-08). Refs #9099.
