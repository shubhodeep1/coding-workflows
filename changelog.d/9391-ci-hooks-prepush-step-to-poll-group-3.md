- **The `Pre-push affected-test gate tests` CI step moved out of `tests-hooks-and-orchestrator`.** That job took 15m32s of its 20-minute timeout on `main` (run 43220859648, budget-watch issue #9391). A job that reaches its timeout turns CI red even when the code is fine. #9078's three-step move out of the same job did not lower its total, so this time the job's largest step that had never moved and is not split was picked. It now runs in `orchestrate-poll`, pinned to matrix group 3 (`if: strategy.job-index == 3`), the group with the lightest pinned load since #9037 moved two of its steps to group 4. The step runs unchanged, under the same name, once per CI run; it needs only temporary git repositories, no Node, no git history beyond `HEAD` and no prepared `.claude/` live copies. The run's step timings could not be read, so the pick is an estimate from test and subprocess counts, not a measurement. No job was added or renamed, no `timeout-minutes` changed, the matrix size is unchanged and `CI / lint` is unchanged; operators have nothing to do. If any job, including `orchestrate-poll (3)`, comes near its timeout, the next `budget-watch` run on `main` opens an issue for it. `tests/test_ci_poll_test_sharding.py` now checks that the step runs once, in group 3 only, after the poll job's setup, has left the hooks job, and still names its ruff check and every module.
