- **`gh_retry` reports a non-zero status when its reader closes the pipe early, on ubuntu-26.04 runners as well.** `scripts/gh_helpers.sh` now replays the successful attempt's output with bash's builtin `printf` in a subshell instead of `cat`. The new image's `cat` exited 0 after `head -c 1` closed the pipe, so `tests/test_gh_retry_stdout_isolation.py::test_closed_reader_does_not_rerun_a_successful_command` saw `rc=0`. Output bytes (including NUL bytes and trailing newlines), the other return codes and the no-re-run behaviour are unchanged, and no temp files are left behind. The `os.path.lexists` part of the same issue already shipped (Refs #9200). Refs #9227
