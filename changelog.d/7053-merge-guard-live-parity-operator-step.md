- **The live merged-PR guard hook waits for an operator to copy it from its template.** On PR #6830's head, the parity test `tests/test_claude_template_live_parity.py` found `.claude/hooks/pr_merge_status_guard.py` out of step with its `workflow-templates/.claude/hooks/` twin. What this means for operators: in a trusted checkout, copy `workflow-templates/.claude/hooks/pr_merge_status_guard.py` over `.claude/hooks/pr_merge_status_guard.py` byte for byte and commit it on the branch that carries the drift (PR #6830's head, or `main` if the drift has landed there). Then remove the `agents.md` note. Until then the parity test keeps reporting the drift. Refs #6832. Refs #6830.
