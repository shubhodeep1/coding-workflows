- **The review step's `REVIEW_HEAD_CHECKOUT` line reports `fetch_ms` and `switch_ms` again on ubuntu-26.04 runners.** `scripts/review_autofix_step_checkout_pr_head_branch.sh` now reads the clock from bash's `EPOCHREALTIME` first. On the new image, `date +%s%3N` (Rust coreutils) gave readings that went backwards, so the elapsed times came out negative and the line printed `na`. That failed `tests/test_review_checkout_head_diagnostics.py::test_head_moved_after_switch_reports_skipped_stale` in CI. The line format is unchanged, `date` is still the fallback for bash < 5, and a new regression test fakes a backwards `date` clock.
