<!-- agents: section="Workflow architecture" -->
**Promote job honours the stable rollback hold (issue #10076, follow-up to #9342).** The `promote` job of `.github/workflows/promote-main-to-stable.yml` (manual `workflow_dispatch`, or the poller's dispatch with `target_sha`) runs the step "Check stable rollback hold" (`id: stable_hold_gate`) directly after "Validate stable is fast-forwardable to main" and before "Fast-forward stable to main" (`id: ff`). It stages `scripts/stable_hold.sh` (plus `gh_helpers.sh` / `emit_event.sh` when present) from `origin/main` into a temp directory and runs `read` with `GH_TOKEN: ${{ github.token }}`: the job checks out `stable`, which may not carry the reader yet, and a missing reader would read as `unknown` and skip every promotion. `inactive` writes `skip=false`. `active <v>` (semver-checked) and anything else (`unknown`, empty output, a reader that cannot be staged) print a `::warning::`, log `PROMOTE_SKIPPED`, write `skip=true` and exit 0 (fail closed). The `ff`, "Resolve version (compute next minor if not provided)", "Dispatch test-and-mark-stable on stable" and "Summary" steps carry `if: steps.stable_hold_gate.outputs.skip != 'true'`, so a held run ends `success` with `stable` and the tags untouched. A skipped poller promotion is handled by the existing hold cap (`COMPREHENSIVE_PROMOTION_HOLD_MAX_SECS`); the poller is unchanged. Kill switch `PROMOTE_STABLE_HOLD_GATE_ENABLED` (default `true`; only `false`, any letter case, turns it off). To promote a fix after a rollback, clear the hold by hand (`"active": false`) or set the switch to `false` for that run. Tests: `tests/test_promote_main_to_stable_push_retry.py::PromoteStableHoldGateTest`.
