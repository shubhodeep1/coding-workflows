- **Run-cancelled skip for `ci/final` (Refs #10131).** When a final CI run is cancelled before `lint` starts, `lint` still runs (`always()`), "Require every CI job to succeed" fails on the cancelled needed jobs and `cancelled()` is false inside the job. Before posting `failure`, `scripts/ci_final_status.sh` (`ci_final_status_run_was_cancelled`) checks `CI_FINAL_STATUS_NEEDS_JSON` (`toJSON(needs)`): with a `cancelled` result, no `failure` result, `GITHUB_RUN_ATTEMPT=1` and `CI_FINAL_STATUS_RUN_CANCELLED_SKIP_ENABLED=true` (default), it runs `scripts/ci_cancelled_rerun.py classify-cancelled-run` (ignoring the `lint`, `final-ci-failed` and `budget-watch` jobs). `classify_cancelled_run` reuses `list_run_jobs`, `failure_annotations` and `shutdown_only_annotations`: a cancelled job that never started (no step ran, no `runner_id`) is `run_cancelled` with no annotation read; otherwise at most `MAX_FAILED_JOBS_CLASSIFIED` cancelled jobs have their annotations read, and any annotation other than the runner-shutdown text or "The operation was canceled." (a `timeout-minutes` cancellation leaves one) is `not_cancelled`. Only `run_cancelled` skips the post; every other verdict and every error posts `failure` as before. `lint` gained `checks: read` for the annotation reads, and its sparse checkout now includes `scripts/ci_cancelled_rerun.py`. Tests: `tests/test_ci_final_status.py`, `tests/test_ci_cancelled_rerun.py`.
