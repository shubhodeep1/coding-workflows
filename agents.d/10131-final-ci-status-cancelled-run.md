- **Run-cancelled skip for `ci/final` (Refs #10131).** When a final CI run is cancelled before `lint` starts, `lint` still runs (`always()`), "Require every CI job to succeed" fails on the cancelled needed jobs and `cancelled()` is false inside the job. Before posting `failure`, `scripts/ci_final_status.sh` (`ci_final_status_run_was_cancelled`) checks `CI_FINAL_STATUS_NEEDS_JSON` (`toJSON(needs)`): with a `cancelled` result, no `failure` result and `CI_FINAL_STATUS_RUN_CANCELLED_SKIP_ENABLED=true` (default), it runs `scripts/ci_cancelled_rerun.py classify-cancelled-run --attempt "${GITHUB_RUN_ATTEMPT}"` (ignoring the `lint`, `final-ci-failed` and `budget-watch` jobs); `list_run_jobs` gained an `attempt` keyword (default 1, so the sweep and `classify-run` still read attempt 1), and the jobs read is retried once after 2 s. `classify_cancelled_run` reuses `list_run_jobs`, `failure_annotations` and `shutdown_only_annotations`: a cancelled job that never started (no step ran, no `runner_id`) is `run_cancelled` with no annotation read; otherwise at most `MAX_FAILED_JOBS_CLASSIFIED` cancelled jobs have their annotations read, and any annotation other than the runner-shutdown text, "The operation was canceled." or a run-level cancel text (`RUN_CANCELLED_MESSAGES`: "The run was canceled by @<user>.", "Canceling since a higher priority waiting request for '<group>' exists") is `not_cancelled` (a `timeout-minutes` cancellation leaves one). Only `run_cancelled` skips the post; every other verdict and every error posts `failure` as before. `lint` gained `checks: read` for the annotation reads, and its sparse checkout now includes `scripts/ci_cancelled_rerun.py`. Tests: `tests/test_ci_final_status.py`, `tests/test_ci_cancelled_rerun.py`.

- `CI_FINAL_STATUS` also logs `sha=<sha> outcome=skipped reason=run_cancelled` (the run was cancelled as a whole; no `ci/final` post) and `sha=<sha> check=run_cancelled verdict=run_cancelled|not_cancelled|unverified|none`, with `verdict=none reason=timeout` when the classifier hits its 90 s limit (`scripts/ci_final_status.sh`, issue #10131).
