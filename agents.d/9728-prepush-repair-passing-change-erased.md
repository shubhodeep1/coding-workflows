**Repair that erases a passing change (#9728, re-issue of #9519).** `review_prepush_test_gate_repair` in `scripts/review_autofix_step_prepush_test_gate.sh` saves the uncommitted change list before the editor's repair turn. After the existing `lint_only_change_erased` guard, a second guard reverts the repair (`review_prepush_test_gate_revert all`), logs `PREPUSH_TEST_GATE ... outcome=repair_reverted reason=passing_change_erased` and sets `rpg_repair` to `reverted (it removed the whole change while no failure was caused by it)` when all of these hold: the change list is empty after the turn, it was not empty before, and `pr_caused` (field 2 of the embedded helper's `status` on the gate result) is 0. An unreadable status skips the guard. In practice it fires only in the repair loop (`PERSIST_REPAIR_LOOP_ENABLED=true`) on a done-check-only repair, which then stops with `stop=reverted`.
- Every editor turn also logs one `PREPUSH_TEST_GATE ... outcome=repair_diff before=<N> after=<N> paths=<P>` line: the counts of the change list before and after the turn, and the first 10 post-turn paths, each stripped to `[A-Za-z0-9._/-]` and joined with commas (`P` may be empty). Tests: `tests/test_review_prepush_test_gate.py` (`test_review_loop_reverts_a_repair_that_erases_a_passing_change`, `test_repair_diff_line_caps_and_sanitises_paths`).
