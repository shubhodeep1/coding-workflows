Compressed state chains (`ORCH_STATE_COMPRESS`, default `false`). `scripts/orchestrate_state_v2.py pack` zlib-compresses the state (level 9) when `--compress` is passed or `ORCH_STATE_COMPRESS` is true, and base64-encodes `zlib:` + the compressed bytes instead of the raw JSON. The opener `<!-- ORCHESTRATOR_STATE_V2 part=N/M manifest=<64hex> -->`, the closer and the manifest (sha256 of the raw, uncompressed state) are byte-for-byte as before, so the poller's jq filters, `prune-plan` and `_ORCHESTRATOR_STATE_V2_COMMENT_RE` in `scripts/workflow_failure_heal.py` match either form. `extract` and `prune-plan` inflate a payload that starts with `zlib:` with at most 8 MiB of output (`MAX_DECOMPRESSED_STATE_BYTES`); a corrupt, truncated, oversized or trailing-data stream is dropped like a hash mismatch and the walk continues to older chains. Readers always accept both forms; the variable only gates the writer (`post_state_comment` in `scripts/orchestrate_poll_process.sh` and the initial post in `orchestrate.yml`). It must stay `false` until every consumer pin in `.github/ai/consumer_repos.json` carries this reader: a reader that cannot parse a newer chain falls back to an older one or to none, and no valid state makes the poller run `rebuild_tracking_state`. `post_state_comment`'s unchanged-content skip hashes the state JSON, not the payload, so it works in both modes. Measured on tracking #8291 (472 KB compact state, 442 KB of it `merged_issue_fingerprints`): 84 KB compressed, 112 KB base64, 2 parts instead of 12.
