<!-- agents: section="Workflow architecture" -->
Heal intake reproduction (P2 step 4 of `docs/plans/heal-evidence-reproduction-and-trends-plan.md`, #8490): with `WORKFLOW_HEAL_REPRODUCE_ENABLED=true` (default `false`; the intake defaults it itself, and both the `intake` and `recovery-scan` jobs of `.github/workflows/workflow-failure-heal-intake.yml` pass the repo variable together with `WORKFLOW_HEAL_REPRODUCE_TIMEOUT_MINUTES`, default 10) `scripts/workflow_failure_heal_intake.sh` runs `_heal_run_reproduction` right after `_heal_build_evidence_pack`, so after `scope_inputs_frozen.json` is read-only and before the diagnosis model; nothing it produces feeds the scope marker, the fingerprint or the diagnosis prompt. It applies to self-repo `workflow_run` reports with a 40-hex `HEAD_SHA` only (`reason=not_applicable` otherwise). `workflow_failure_heal.py reproduction-tests` turns `FAILING_TESTS_JSON` into the `tests/<path>.py[::name]` list (`reproduction_test_list`: `pairs` first, then uncovered `files`, strict ID regex, bare names dropped, cap `2 * FAILING_TEST_LIMIT`); an empty list skips with `reason=no_tests` and makes no API call. The baseline comes from `last_green_run(repo, workflow_id, branch, *, failing_sha, before, run_gh=None)` in `scripts/workflow_failure_heal.py` (CLI `last-green-run`), a reusable helper for the later trend and suspect-commit phases: one single-attempt `GET repos/<repo>/actions/workflows/<id>/runs?status=success&branch=<b>&per_page=1` (zero on invalid input), counted in `GH_PAT_BUDGET`, fail-open (`request_failed`, `malformed`, `no_success_run`, and `same_sha` / `newer_than_failure` when the green run is the failing commit or was created at or after the failing run). The workflow id and the failing run's `created_at` come from the saved `${LOG_DIR}/run-<id>-info.json`. Any reason other than `ok` gives `unverified` / `no_baseline` without running the helper. Otherwise `scripts/heal_reproduce.sh` (next to the intake, overridable for tests with `WORKFLOW_HEAL_REPRODUCE_SCRIPT`) runs with `--sha HEAD_SHA --baseline <green sha>` under an outer `timeout` 5 minutes above its wall and without `GH_TOKEN`, `GITHUB_TOKEN`, `TG_*` or `OPENROUTER_API_KEY`; a missing helper, no result file or the outer timeout give `unverified` with `helper_missing`, `helper_failed` or `timeout`. `reproduction-normalize` validates the `heal_repro.v1` result (`normalize_reproduction_result`; anything invalid becomes `unverified` / `result_invalid`), and `compose-issue --reproduction-file` renders `## Reproduction` (`render_reproduction_section`: an untrusted-data banner, then the intake-authored lines `status: <status>`, `reason: <reason>`, `failing_at: <sha|none>`, `passing_at: <sha|none>`, then one bullet per test) after the evidence pack and before the diagnosis, through `sanitize_text` and `neutralize_untrusted_routing`, ahead of `validate_heal_issue_body_routing`. The `status: reproduced` line is what makes the `HEAL_COUNTERFACTUAL: REPRODUCIBLE` option available to the planner (only the section before the diagnosis is intake-authored). Only `reproduced` changes the classification: a `transient` verdict becomes `inconclusive` with a heal intake note; `pre_existing`, `passes_at_failing_sha` and `unverified` are shown and change nothing. With the switch off the body and the API calls are byte-identical to before. API budget: at most 1 extra GET per report. The last-green GET adds `created=<<failing run created_at>` so a green run newer than the failure is never returned. The intake clamps the reproduction wall to 1-20 minutes (passed to the helper as `WORKFLOW_HEAL_REPRODUCE_TIMEOUT_MINUTES`, with `GIT_TERMINAL_PROMPT=0`), and the `intake` job's `timeout-minutes` is now 156 (was 130) to cover it; reproduction never runs in `recovery-scan` (autofix-failure replays only), whose 175 minutes are unchanged.
