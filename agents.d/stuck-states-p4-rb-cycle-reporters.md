- **Promote-cycle release-blocker reporters (stuck-states P4 / release-blocker Phase 2b, Refs #8291).** `scripts/promote_main_cycle.sh` (the `cycle` job of `.github/workflows/promote-main-to-stable.yml`, daily) calls `release_blocker_on_skip` as the first statement of `skip_cycle`, so at most one `release_blocker` heal report per tick goes out before the unchanged `PROMOTE_CYCLE_SKIPPED` line, `GITHUB_OUTPUT` and exit code: `cycle_gate_unavailable` when this tick and the previous `RELEASE_BLOCKER_CYCLE_SKIP_STREAK`-1 (default 3, range 1-10) completed scheduled ticks all skipped `gate_busy` / `smoke_gate_cancelled` (earlier reasons read from each run's `cycle` job log, 1 jobs + 1 logs GET per run, only when all of them concluded `success`); `cycle_held_untrusted_marker` on a `cycle_in_flight` skip when a held tracking issue (first 5) carries a trusted `## ⚠️ Untrusted apply-analysis marker` alert from the poller at least `RELEASE_BLOCKER_UNTRUSTED_HOLD_SECS` (86400) old with no trusted baseline marker after it (one batched GraphQL comments read, `release_blocker_untrusted_comments`; a parity test pins the heading to `orchestrate_poll_process.sh`); and `cycle_stale`, only when neither reported and main is ahead of the `stable` tag with code, when `max(last baseline-marker time, oldest unreleased commit)` is `RELEASE_BLOCKER_CYCLE_STALE_SECS` (259200) old. Reports use `head_branch=main`, no `run_refs` (named runs go in the evidence only), the OIDC identity (`id-token: write` on the `cycle` job) and the same `RELEASE_BLOCKER_REPORTED` / `RELEASE_BLOCKER_REPORT_SKIPPED` lines as Phase 2a. The reporter sets no EXIT trap (`pin_release_freeze` owns it) and removes its work dir through `release_blocker_cleanup`. `fail_cycle` is not hooked: failed cycle runs already reach the intake through `workflow_run`. `RELEASE_BLOCKER_HEAL_ENABLED=false` makes no detection read and sends nothing.
