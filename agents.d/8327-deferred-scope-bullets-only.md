Deferred scope holds only bullets (issue #8327): `partial_delivery.py parse_plan` rejects any non-bullet line inside `## Deferred scope` (`prose_in_block`; an indented line still continues the bullet above it), so a planner's trailing note made a heal plan's `HEAL_COUNTERFACTUAL: PARTIAL` fail with `deferred_scope_missing` and would have closed a partial delivery at merge. Rule 12 of the plan prompt (`prompts/mode-plan.txt`, `prompts/_templates/mode-plan.txt`, the inline copy in `scripts/run_plan_codex.sh`) and `prompts/mode-plan-heal-counterfactual.txt` now say the section holds only `- ` bullets and that notes go in another section. The `deferred_scope_missing` hold comment from `plan.yml`'s "Auto-approve clear plan" step and the poller's `_plan_auto_approval_gate` adds that the section must appear once and hold only `- ` bullets. The parser is unchanged. Tests: `tests/test_plan_heal_counterfactual_gate.py` (fixture `tests/fixtures/heal_counterfactual/issue_8327_plan.md`), `tests/test_partial_delivery.py`.
