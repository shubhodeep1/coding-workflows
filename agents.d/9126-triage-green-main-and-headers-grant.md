Plan-time check-triage resolution (consumer fun-token-multi-chain #765): when "Close superseded CI-repair issue (merged PR)" gets `merged_check_not_passed` and would plan on the PR's base as usual, it first runs the support copy of `check_triage_resolution.py gate` against the issue. `resolved` or `already_resolved` (the gate posts the evidence comment and closes the issue) sets `SKIP_PLAN=true` and logs `PLAN_SUPERSEDED_MERGED_PR ... outcome=resolved reason=default_branch_green gate=<status>`. Exit 3 (verified, but the close failed) also skips planning with `reason=resolved_close_failed`. Any other result plans as before and logs `gate=<status|none> gate_rc=<n|not_run>`; `not_run` means the gate was skipped (helper missing or a symlink, or an invalid default branch), and a non-zero exit other than 3 also prints a `::warning::` with the gate's last stderr lines. `CHECK_TRIAGE_RESOLVED_GATE_ENABLED=false` turns the check off. Re-issues (no `ai:check-triage` label) return `not_triage` and plan as before.
