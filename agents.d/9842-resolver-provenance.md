<!-- agents: section="Workflow architecture" -->
- **Resolver provenance check (issue #9842, plan Phase 3 of `docs/plans/pipeline-human-wait-elimination-plan.md`).** `scripts/review_conflict_resolve.sh` rejects a resolution whose regions match neither side of the conflict.
  - *Capture.* `_capture_resolution_provenance_stages` runs once, after the unmerged index is written to `RESOLVER_UNMERGED_INDEX_FILE` and before the first model attempt. It copies the stage 1/2/3 blobs (`git cat-file blob`) of every allowlisted regular-file path with both stage 2 and stage 3 to `RESOLVER_PROVENANCE_STAGES_DIR` (under `RUNTIME_DIR`) plus `manifest.json`. Paired live copies (`RESOLVER_PAIRED_LIVE_FILE`), modify/delete paths and paths prepare already resolved never enter the manifest. A capture error logs `reason=capture_failed` and skips the check (fail open).
  - *Verify.* `_verify_resolution_provenance` runs inside the soft-validation success branch, after markers and fingerprints pass. Per path it diffs the two-sided merge (`git -c merge.conflictStyle=merge merge-file -p`; not `--diff3`, whose base section would hide a restored base-only line) against the resolved file (`git diff --no-index -U0`) and calls a hunk foreign when any added line, whitespace-normalised, is non-empty and absent from both "ours" and "theirs". Foreign hunks (path, resolved line range, at most 40 added lines each, 16 KiB total) go only to `RESOLVER_PROVENANCE_REPORT_FILE`, never to the log. Symlinks, non-regular files, NUL-containing and over-2 MiB blobs are skipped.
  - *Retry and escalation.* A foreign result sets `_prev_attempt_failure_kind="provenance"`; `_build_retry_prompt` renders `prompts/conflict-resolver-retry-provenance-prelude.txt` (staged by `scripts/stage_workflow_support.sh`; an inline header plus the report when absent) with outcome `provenance-prelude`. Once failures exceed `RESOLVER_PROVENANCE_RETRY_MAX` (default `1`, clamped 0..1) or on the last attempt, the resolver writes `CONFLICT_RESOLVED=false`, sets `RESOLVER_TERMINAL_EXIT_CLASS="provenance"` (new `RESOLVER_TERMINAL exit_class=provenance` value) and exits 1 through the existing EXIT trap.
  - *Switch / rollback.* `RESOLVER_PROVENANCE_CHECK_ENABLED` defaults `true` in the script and in the resolver step's `env:` of `.github/workflows/review_autofix.yml`; only `false` (any case) turns it off. Tests: `tests/test_review_conflict_resolve_provenance.py`.
