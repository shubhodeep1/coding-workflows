- **Fix-up closed as already implemented ends the unblock wait (#8191).** The fix-up wait in `scripts/unblock_judge.sh` ends on a fix-up closed `completed` with `ai:merged`, as before, and also on one closed `completed` with `ai:closed` whose comments include one by the pipeline login (`UNBLOCK_LOGIN`) ending with `<!-- ai:implement-success-noop:v1 issue=<fixup> -->`. `.github/workflows/implement.yml` Guard 0 (success-no-op) appends that marker to its close comment. `unblock_fixup_success_noop` does the check with one paginated comments read, only in the `ai:closed` case. A match logs `item=<n> fixup=<f> op=fixup_done via=success_noop` and runs the same follow-up, hold and handoff paths as a merge, with "closed as already implemented" in place of "merged" in their wait comments and, through `unblock_actions.py followup-handoff --done-text` (default `merged`, passed only in this case), in the protected-path handoff comment, operator step and WARNING. Guard 0 logs a `::warning::` when neither close-comment attempt posts. An unreadable comment list logs `outcome=skip reason=fixup_comments_unavailable` and leaves the wait open. A missing or untrusted marker still reports "did not merge in time" and judges again.
