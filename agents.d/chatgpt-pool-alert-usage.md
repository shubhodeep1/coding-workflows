- **ChatGPT keys on the Claude pool alert and report lines (ChatGPT trial plan Phase 4, Refs #8969).** `CLAUDE_POOL_HEALTH ... waiting=<n> chatgpt_accounts=<n> chatgpt_alerts=<n>` and `CLAUDE_POOL_DAILY_USAGE op=send ... truncated=<true|false> chatgpt_accounts=<n> chatgpt_alerts=<n>`: the keys are appended last and every existing key keeps its place. `chatgpt_accounts` counts the valid accounts in the `chatgpt-pool-token` `mode: status` output (`CHATGPT_POOL_STATUS`), and `chatgpt_alerts` counts those with a cause. The causes are: login rejected (`last_outcome=auth_failed`, names `CHATGPT_POOL_AUTH_<NAME>`); last refresh failed (any other outcome except `ok` / `quota_exhausted`); stale (`last_refresh` missing, naive or at least `CHATGPT_POOL_ALERT_STALE_HOURS`, default `12`, old); usage at or above the broker gate; quota exhausted. The first three make the hourly message `WARNING`, while the gate and quota causes alone keep it `DEBUG`. ChatGPT lines go out only in the Claude alert's hourly window. `CHATGPT_POOL_HEALTH_ALERT_ENABLED=false` drops them, and `CLAUDE_POOL_HEALTH_ALERT_ENABLED=false` still disables the whole step. Claude probes `[]` with no ChatGPT account still log `alert=no_probes`. The daily report shows `ChatGPT accounts: unavailable (<status_invalid|action reason>).` when the status could not be read, and nothing when the pool has no accounts. The lines never carry a token or the status JSON: `claude_engine.py` reads only whitelisted fields and character-checks every rendered string.

- **ChatGPT pool status steps in `orchestrate_poll.yml` (ChatGPT trial plan Phase 4, Refs #8969).** "Read ChatGPT pool status" (`id: chatgpt_pool_status`, `continue-on-error: true`) runs `.github/actions/chatgpt-pool-token` with `mode: status` (`GET /v1/chatgpt/status`; it writes no credential). It appears twice. In the `poll` job it runs right before "Alert on Claude pool accounts at the usage gate", under the same `if:` plus `vars.CHATGPT_POOL_HEALTH_ALERT_ENABLED != 'false'`, and the alert gets `CHATGPT_POOL_STATUS`, `CHATGPT_POOL_HEALTH_ALERT_ENABLED` and `CHATGPT_POOL_ALERT_STALE_HOURS`. In the `claude_pool_daily_usage` job it runs between "Probe the Claude pool" and "Send Claude pool daily usage report", under the probe's `if:`, and the send step gets `CHATGPT_POOL_STATUS`, `CHATGPT_POOL_STATUS_REASON` (the action's `reason`, `not_read` when the step was skipped) and `CHATGPT_POOL_ALERT_STALE_HOURS`. `orchestrate_poll.yml` is already in the broker's `ALLOWED_WORKFLOW_FILES`. Contract tests: `tests/test_chatgpt_pool_health_alert.py` and `tests/test_claude_pool_daily_usage.py`.
