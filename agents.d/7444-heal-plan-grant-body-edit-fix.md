Heal-issue plan approval and pipeline-only body edits (#7444, unblock fix-up for #7434): with `HEAL_PLAN_GRANT_BODY_EDIT_FIX_ENABLED` on (unset, empty or `true`; `false` turns it off, any other value turns it off with a warning), plan.yml's "Auto-approve clear plan" never runs the Q108 pipeline-grant PATCH for an `ai:workflow-heal` issue. Approval reason `heal_scope_verified`; holds `heal_scope_unverified`, `heal_scope_plan_out_of_scope`, `heal_scope_plan_paths_missing`, `heal_scope_check_unavailable`; log `PIPELINE_PLAN_GRANT ... outcome=skipped reason=heal_issue`. Refusals keep `reason=edited` with `detail=edit_history_unavailable|edit_history_redacted|editor_untrusted|marker_changed|unknown`. The poller's `_plan_auto_approval_gate` still writes Q108 grants into heal issues; the preflight accepts those pipeline-only edits.
