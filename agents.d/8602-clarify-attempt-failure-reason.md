Clarify attempt failure line (#8602, heal of shubhodeep1/btc_sweeper#248). The "Run Codex" step of `clarify.yml` copies the Claude call's stderr to `${RUNTIME_DIR}/clarify_attempt_stderr.txt` (an `exec` redirect through `tee`, so the job log still gets it live; the file is truncated at the start of every attempt, and the tee wait is capped at 10 seconds). After the existing `::warning::` line of a failed or empty attempt it prints `CLARIFY_ENGINE_ATTEMPT_FAILED engine=<Claude|Codex> attempt= max_attempts= kind=nonzero_exit|empty_output exit_code= pool_role= pool_account= pool_outcome= pool_reason= pool_exit_code=`, taken from the last `CLAUDE_POOL run` line of that attempt. Only those five keys are read, each value is stripped to `[A-Za-z0-9_.-]` and cut to 64 characters, and missing values (no line, or the Codex path after a fallback) read `unknown`. The line exists because the heal intake's `filter_log` drops `##[warning]` lines and the `CLAUDE_POOL run` line but keeps `*_FAILED` lines. Exit codes, retries and the existing messages are unchanged. Tests: the `clarify_attempt` cases in `tests/test_plan_clarify_blocked_output.py`.
