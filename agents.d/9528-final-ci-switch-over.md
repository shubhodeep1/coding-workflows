**Final CI switch-over (cut-ci-runner-time Phase 4, step 2 of 2, Refs #9528).** The mechanism described under "Final CI at the end of review" is on in this repository:
- `ci.yml` has no `pull_request` trigger. `.github/workflows/ci_pr_quick.yml` (`name: CI quick`, `pull_request` `[opened, synchronize, reopened, ready_for_review]` for every base, concurrency `ci-quick-pr-<n>` with cancellation) has one job, `quick`, which calls `./.github/workflows/ci.yml` with `mode: quick`. Its permissions are the union of what `ci.yml`'s jobs declare, because a called job may not ask for more than its caller grants even when it is skipped. Its check-runs are `quick / <job>`, so a skipped `quick / lint` can never satisfy the required `lint`.
- `internal-review.yml` passes `final_ci_workflow: ci.yml` to `review_autofix.yml@promote-candidate`. It only shipped after `promote-candidate` carried the input, because passing an input the called workflow does not define fails every review run. The review sweep step "Ensure the final CI run on reviewed PR heads" sets `REVIEW_FINAL_CI_WORKFLOW: ci.yml`. Both must name the same file.
- `CI quick` runs are not `CI` runs: `AUTO_MERGE_WAIT_WORKFLOWS` (default `CI`), check-failure triage (`workflow_run` of `["CI"]`) and the cancelled-CI rerun sweep ignore them. Their failures reach the autofix through the review's check-run context.
- Rollback: revert the switch-over change. Tests: `tests/test_ci_pr_quick.py`.
