- Job duration in `scripts/ci_budget_watch.py` (#9559) runs from the job's first started step to its `completed_at`, so time spent waiting for a runner is not counted. A finished job whose `steps` list has no started step (for example one cancelled while queued) is not reported and logs `::notice::ci_budget_watch: job <name> ran no step (conclusion=<c>); duration not checked.` instead. A job that hits its timeout ran its steps, so it is still reported although GitHub marks it `cancelled`. When the jobs response has no `steps` list, the job's own `started_at` is used. A job still queued (no `completed_at`) is skipped without a notice. A finished job whose start or completion time is missing or does not parse logs `::notice::ci_budget_watch: job <name> has no usable start or completion time (conclusion=<c>); duration not checked.` instead of a finding.
