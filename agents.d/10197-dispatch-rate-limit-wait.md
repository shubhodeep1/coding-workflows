- **Dispatch rate-limit wait (#10197).** `dispatch_workflow()` in `scripts/dispatch_and_watch_workflow_run.sh` captures the stderr of `gh workflow run` (and still prints it). When the text matches the `_is_gh_rate_limit` regex (matched locally as `DISPATCH_WATCH_RATE_LIMIT_RE`, because this script never sources `scripts/gh_helpers.sh`; `_is_gh_rate_limit` is used when defined, and `tests/test_dispatch_and_watch_rate_limit.py` keeps the two regexes equal) and `DISPATCH_RATE_LIMIT_MAX_WAIT_SECS` (default `1500`, `0` = off, a non-integer warns and uses `1500`) is above 0, it waits instead of spending one of `--dispatch-max-attempts`: 60 s on secondary-limit text, otherwise one uncounted `GET /rate_limit` with the step's `GH_TOKEN` gives `core.reset - now + 5` when `core.remaining` is 0, 60 s (`kind=secondary`) when quota is left, and 60 s (`kind=primary`) when the read fails. The cap counts the larger of the summed waits and the wall-clock time since `DISPATCH_RATE_LIMIT_CLOCK_START_EPOCH`, taken before the pre-dispatch run-id lookup, so that lookup's `gh_api_safe` backoff (up to 30+60+120 s on the same PAT) and the `/rate_limit` reads stay inside the cap. A wait that would take that time past the cap logs `elapsed_s=<n> outcome=cap_reached`, prints the unchanged `::error::gh workflow run <file> failed after <N> attempts` line and fails without sleeping. Other failures keep the attempt count and `DISPATCH_BACKOFF_BASE_SECS ** attempt` backoff. After a wait, the fallback registration window (used when the pre-dispatch lookup failed) starts when dispatching resumed (`DISPATCH_RATE_LIMIT_RESUME_EPOCH`), not before the wait. In `.github/workflows/test-and-mark-stable.yml` every dispatch step uses a `1500` cap (set through `env:`, or the default for the workflow-log-analysis snapshot), and each step's `timeout-minutes` (or, for the orchestrate dispatch, its job limit) was raised so cap + watch deadline + 4 s pre-sleep + 60 s margin fits it (issue #10205). `test_gate_dispatch_steps_fit_rate_limit_cap_in_step_budget` enforces the budget rule.

- `DISPATCH_RATE_LIMIT_WAIT` — `workflow=<file> kind=<primary|secondary> wait_s=<n> waited_total_s=<n> max_s=<n> [elapsed_s=<n> outcome=cap_reached]`, one line per rate-limit wait of `gh workflow run` in `scripts/dispatch_and_watch_workflow_run.sh` (issue #10197); `waited_total_s` includes the wait, except on `outcome=cap_reached`, where it is the time already waited, `elapsed_s` is the time counted against the cap (waits or wall-clock since the pre-dispatch lookup began, whichever is larger) and no sleep follows.
