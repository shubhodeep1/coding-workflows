- **Editor stall fallback.** In the editor retry loop of `scripts/review_apply_fixes.sh`, an attempt the stall guard killed (`stall_state=killed`) sets `editor_prev_attempt_stall_killed`, and the next attempt runs on `MODEL_EDITOR_FALLBACK` instead of `MODEL_EDITOR` (`editor_stall_fallback_applies`). It switches only when `MODEL_EDITOR_FALLBACK` is set and differs from `MODEL_EDITOR`, `${AI_ENGINE_RESOLVED_REVIEW_EDITOR:-codex}` is not `claude` (the Claude sandbox ignores the OpenRouter slug), and `EDITOR_STALL_FALLBACK_ENABLED` is on: unset, empty or `true` (any letter case) is on, `false` is off, and any other value is off with one `::warning::`, read once inside the script with no `:?` guard. Once switched, the remaining attempts stay on the fallback. It logs the plain line `Editor attempt N: previous attempt was stall-killed; switching model to fallback <model>.` with no new prefix. The final-attempt capacity fallback, retries after failures that are not stall kills, and the exit 78, 79 and 80 branches are unchanged. `tests/test_editor_capacity_fallback_contract.py` pins the condition and checks the selection behaviour.
