### The release-gate dispatcher cleans up fixtures and backs off on a deep runner queue before a retry

`scripts/auto_release_stable.sh` (run every 6 hours by `.github/workflows/auto-release-stable.yml`) dispatches `test-and-mark-stable.yml` when `stable` is ahead of its tag, and retries a failed gate up to `AUTO_RELEASE_STABLE_MAX_ATTEMPTS` times per tip. Between 2026-11-10 and 2026-11-17 the gate ran 16 times and passed twice. The failed cycles (43445515854, 43590943288, 43708007145, 43878140217, 43934729786) timed out in `Phase 1: Wait for clarify to complete` and the sibling E2E waits because the child runs sat in the Actions queue, and each retry filed its own five to nine fixture issues and PRs into that queue (79 issues and 29 PRs in the window). Two steps now run after the attempts-budget check, in this order:

- **Fixture cleanup.** When the gate-run listing the script already read holds a completed run whose conclusion is not `success` (any branch, so the promote cycle's `gate_only` runs and cancelled runs count), the script reads the token's login (one `user` call) and runs two searches (open issues, open PRs with `[E2E` in the title, `author:<login>`, without the two orchestrator labels; one page of 100 each). A result is closed only when its title starts with `[E2E `, names one of those runs as `(run <id>)` or `E2E Orchestrate Smoke <id>`, was written by that login and carries neither `ai:orchestrator-tracking` nor `ai:orchestrator-managed`; the script re-checks author and labels itself and does not rely on the search qualifiers. Orchestrator projects stay with the poller's `SMOKE_FIXTURE_REAP`, which closes a project together with its children. A fixture issue's pipeline PR (`AI implementation for issue #<N>`, label `e2e-smoke-test`) is closed by the review sweep's smoke-PR backstop on its next tick once the issue is closed. Issues close as `not_planned`, PRs close; at most `AUTO_RELEASE_STABLE_FIXTURE_CLEANUP_MAX` (default `30`) per tick. Nothing is deleted and no comment is posted, because eleven workflows start on `issue_comment` and this runs when the queue is already deep; the log line is the record. Failure modes: a failed `user` read or search logs `outcome=read_failed` and closes nothing; a failed close logs `outcome=close_failed` and the next tick retries it; the dispatch decision goes ahead in every case. With `github.token` in place of `GH_PAT` the closes fail (the job grants no `issues: write`) and are only logged. Switch: `AUTO_RELEASE_STABLE_FIXTURE_CLEANUP_ENABLED=false`.
- **Runner-queue back-off.** The newest completed gate run on `stable` that was not cancelled decides. When it ended in `failure` or `timed_out` less than `AUTO_RELEASE_STABLE_QUEUE_BACKOFF_HOURS` (default `24`) ago, the script reads the repository's queued-run count (`actions/runs?status=queued&per_page=1`, one call) and skips with `AUTO_RELEASE_SKIPPED reason=runner_queue_deep` while the count is at or above `AUTO_RELEASE_STABLE_MAX_QUEUED_RUNS` (default `100`, `0` disables). A skipped tick spends no attempt. The gate's own jobs can get a runner promptly while its child runs wait, so the signal is the queue, not the gate's job timings. Once the failure is older than the window the tick logs `outcome=not_recent` and dispatches one cycle whatever the queue: a deep queue delays a release (a hotfix on a new tip included) by at most the window, rounded up to the next 6-hour tick, and a failed cycle starts the window again. Only `queued` runs of this repository are counted; `pending` runs wait for a concurrency group, and other repositories' queues are not readable from here. A successful or cancelled newest run, or a run on another branch, costs no extra call. Fail-open: an unreadable count logs `AUTO_RELEASE_QUEUE_CHECK outcome=read_failed` and dispatches.

The daily promote cycle (`scripts/promote_main_cycle.sh`) dispatches its own `gate_only` run and has neither step; its fixtures are covered by the cleanup above on the next auto-release tick that reaches it. A tick that skips earlier (`disabled`, `up_to_date`, `branch_not_ahead`, `release_in_flight`, `last_gate_failed`) does not run the cleanup.
