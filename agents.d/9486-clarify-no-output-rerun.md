- **Standalone clarification with no clarify output (issue #9486).** shubhodeep1/digital_pa#146 sat in `ai:clarification` for 34 days with no clarify comment. In the standalone stall loop of `scripts/orchestrate_poll_process.sh`, after the recovery action is chosen and before the active-workflow, merged-PR, fresh-push and phase-cap guards (which still gate the result), an `ai:clarification` issue whose action is not `skip` is checked by `_standalone_clarify_no_output_state` against its complete comment history (the batched `_candidate_details_json` window when it holds fewer than 100 comments, otherwise the paged read in `_staged_support_comments_for_guard`; when neither is complete the check is skipped with `::warning::[standalone-stall] clarify-output check skipped for issue #N: comment history incomplete` and the legacy action runs). Clarify output is a trusted comment carrying `<!-- ai:clarification-questions -->` or starting `Clarification required`, or a trusted comment with a line starting `/answer`. Trusted means a login ending in `[bot]` or `author_association` OWNER, MEMBER or COLLABORATOR (the `stall_recovery_clarify_answer` list). With output, nothing changes. Without output and without a trusted marker, the action becomes `retrigger_pipeline` and the arm posts `/reclarify` with `<!-- ai:clarify-no-output-rerun:<issue> -->` on the second line (`STALL_RECOVERY issue=N reason=clarify_no_output ... override_from=<action>`). With a trusted marker younger than the phase stall threshold (`STALL_THRESHOLD_CLARIFICATION_MINUTES`, default 60; no separate clock), the tick logs `STALL_SKIP ... reason=clarify_no_output_rerun_pending` and does nothing; once the marker is at least one threshold old, the action becomes `escalate_human` (`STALL_RECOVERY ... reason=clarify_no_output_second_miss`), even with `ENABLE_STALL_HUMAN_TERMINALIZATION=false`. No new environment variables, actions or state fields.
- **Security-dependency hold advisory (issue #9486, AD-2).** A standalone issue held by `security_dependency.py` (unchanged `STALL_SKIP ... reason=security_dependency_held detail=<open|closed_without_ai_merged|unverified>`) stays held, but `_post_security_dependency_held_advisory` posts one comment per (issue, dependency) pair ending in `<!-- ai:security-dependency-held:<issue>:<depends_on> -->`. `depends_on` must match `^[1-9][0-9]{0,9}$`; the advisory is posted only when the comment history already in hand is complete (no extra read), and a trusted marker (same trust list) suppresses it. The `open` body says no action is needed; `closed_without_ai_merged` and `unverified` say a maintainer must act. The body never has a line starting `/reclarify` or `/answer` and never carries the clarification-questions marker. Log: `STALL_SKIP issue=N reason=security_dependency_held_advisory depends_on=<M|none> detail=<D> action=<advisory_posted|advisory_present|advisory_failed|advisory_comments_incomplete|advisory_no_dependency>`. The new reason values use existing prefixes.
