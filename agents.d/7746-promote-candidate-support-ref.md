- **Support code from the protected `promote-candidate` branch (#7746, phase 1 of `docs/plans/pin-promote-cycle-to-candidate-plan.md`, Refs #7745).** In this repository, the support-ref resolve steps of `cancel_on_pr_close`, `check_failure_triage`, `clarify`, `implement`, `issue_pr_status`, `memory_maintenance`, `orchestrate`, `orchestrate_clarify_respond`, `orchestrate_poll`, `plan`, `review_autofix`, `validate` and `workflow_failure_heal` check the job `workflow_ref`. Only when it is exactly `shubhodeep1/coding-workflows/.github/workflows/<this file>.yml@refs/heads/promote-candidate` do they make one `GET branches/promote-candidate` read. They take its tip as the support ref only when the branch is protected and the tip is 40 hex, and log `SUPPORT_REF source=promote_candidate sha=<sha>`. Otherwise they keep the previous source and log `SUPPORT_REF source=promote_candidate_unprotected`. These lines are a second `SUPPORT_REF` shape alongside the consumer `ref=<pin|stable> workflow_ref=<ref>` line. `review_autofix.yml` also accepts `refs/heads/promote-candidate` as a review support ref. The gh-retry bootstrap steps (`validate.yml` included) still clone `main`, because `tests/test_workflow_gh_retry_fallback_contract.py` requires them to stay byte-identical across workflows (one of them is in `security-audit.yml`). Consumer pins and `@main` runs are unchanged.
