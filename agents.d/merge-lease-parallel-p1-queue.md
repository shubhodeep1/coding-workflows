- `MERGE_LEASE_QUEUE` (merge-lease parallel merges Phase 1 steps 1.2-1.4, Refs #9781). `_mt_merge_lease_queue_log` in `scripts/review_merge_train.sh` prints it to stdout once per lease ref per `_mt_merge_lease_tick` pass, after that ref's hold, release or grant decision (none after a record read failure; one per base for the multi-holder tick too). Format: `MERGE_LEASE_QUEUE base=<ref> holder=<n|-> held_s=<s|-> waiters=<count> order=<comma list|->`.
  - `order` is the labelled (`MERGE_LEASE_WAIT_LABEL`), non-draft waiters on the base in lease-queue order (priority label or forward-merge head first, then the lowest PR number), from `_merge_lease_queue_order` in `scripts/pr_checks_lib.sh`. Holders and waiters the tick unlabelled in the same pass (ineligible, granted, or skip-held handed back to the sweep) are left out; skip-held waiters that keep their label stay in. `waiters` is the length of `order`. This is the plan's K2 source: a PR's wait runs from its first to its last appearance in `order=`.
  - `holder` is the primary holder after the decision (`-` when the lease is free); `held_s` is the time since its `acquired=` (`-` without a holder or without `acquired=`).
  - `_merge_lease_queue_first` also sets `MERGE_LEASE_QUEUE_LEN` and `MERGE_LEASE_QUEUE_PRS` (the rows it considered, after the skip and exclude filters, in queue order); its printed output is unchanged. Callers using `$(...)` lose the globals, so the tick reads `_merge_lease_queue_order <pulls> [<skips>] [<exclude_csv>]`, which prints the comma list.
  - Appended `MERGE_LEASE` fields (plan step 1.1, earlier fields unchanged): tick `outcome=released` lines end with ` held_s=<s|-> merged=true|false` (`merged=true` only for a closed holder whose existing `pulls/<n>` read carries `merged_at`); tick `outcome=granted` lines gain ` queue_len=<eligible waiters>` (the queue at grant time, the granted PR included, skip-held and ineligible waiters left out), placed before ` holders=<n>` in the multi-holder tick.
  - No GitHub API call is added. When the staged `pr_checks_lib.sh` predates `_merge_lease_queue_order` (`declare -F`), there is no `MERGE_LEASE_QUEUE` line and no `queue_len=` field.
