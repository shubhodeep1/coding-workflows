- **Review convergence rule (issue #10056).** `review_convergence_classify` in `scripts/review_issue_ledger.sh` runs after the reject verifier and before the ledger (also when `REVIEW_LEDGER_ENABLED=0`). From round `REVIEW_CONVERGENCE_FROM_ROUND` (default `3`, valid `2`-`5`; round = `REVIEW_CONVERGENCE_AUTOFIX_COUNT` from the `retrigger_guard` step's `autofix_iteration` plus one, falling back to `AUTOFIX_ITERATION`) and with at least `REVIEW_CONVERGENCE_MIN_REVIEWERS + 1` non-empty reviewer sections in `reviewer_bundle.txt` (a section whose every non-empty line is a failure or skip notice from `scripts/review_run_reviewers.sh` (`Reviewer [slot] <model> [(<model>)] failed|skipped|stopped` followed by ` after `, ` because `, ` on `, `: ` or ` —`, which covers the budget, circuit-breaker, PR-closed, pool and ChatGPT slot notices) neither counts toward the panel nor supports a finding), an `=== ISSUE` block classified `must-fix`, `nice-to-have`, `unclassified` or empty is reclassified `advisory-lone` (with `CONVERGENCE_PRIOR_CLASSIFICATION`, `CONVERGENCE_REVIEWERS`, `CONVERGENCE_ROUND` lines) when fewer than `REVIEW_CONVERGENCE_MIN_REVIEWERS` (default `2`) reviewer sections support it, it has no `FLOOR_CRITICAL_KEYWORD:*` floor row on the same file within +/-3 lines, and its `LENS` names no security lens (matched case-insensitively by the keywords `SECURITY`, `TRUST-BOUNDARY` or `IMPLICIT-EXECUTION`, so lens 1 and lens 9 and their variants exempt; an empty or `UNKNOWN_LENS` lens also exempts). `FLOOR_HIGH_CONFIDENCE` and `FLOOR_MULTI_REVIEWER` never exempt (AD-1). A section supports a finding with a `path:line` / `FILE:`+`LINE:` anchor, or a `File:` line followed by `Line or code reference:` or `Code:` (every integer or `N-M` range in the reference, at most 20), on the same file and overlapping `LINES` within +/-3 lines (a range counts when it overlaps, so a containing range supports the finding); as in `scripts/review_floor_rules.sh`, a backticked, quoted or annotated `File:` value, an empty `File:` label with the path on the next line, and an empty reference label with the reference on the next non-empty line are accepted, or by containing an `EVIDENCE` quote of at least 24 characters; every supporting section counts whether or not `FLAGGED_BY` names it, and a `FLAGGED_BY` name without file support does not count. Section content is bounded by the bundle's `BYTES:` line, so reviewer text cannot forge a section. A model-written `advisory-lone` is always re-evaluated. The step writes `${RUNTIME_DIR}/review_convergence_advisory.txt` (TSV: block id, `file:lines`, lens, severity, reviewers, round, sanitised summary of at most 200 bytes); after the residual rewrite `review_convergence_stub_advisory_blocks` turns those blocks into `=== ADVISORY ISSUE <id> ===` stubs (a RESIDUAL stub wins). Ledger entries gain optional `CONVERGENCE: advisory-lone`, `CONVERGENCE_ROUND`, `CONVERGENCE_REVIEWERS` lines that `parse_prior_ledger` accepts. Consumers: the editor prompt in `scripts/review_apply_fixes.sh` (block only when the TSV is non-empty; directive when the TSV is non-empty or `review_issues.txt` holds an `=== ADVISORY ISSUE` stub, as on a same-head resume, and only while `REVIEW_CONVERGENCE_ENABLED` is on; the directive never applies to a failure in `PR_CHECK_RUNS_CONTEXT_FILE`), the "Not blocking (one reviewer)" section of `scripts/review_autofix_step_post_editor_summary.sh` (at most 50 rows plus an "and N more" line; rebuilt from the `=== ADVISORY ISSUE` stubs when the TSV is absent, as on a same-head resume; URL schemes and `#` are stripped; nothing while `REVIEW_CONVERGENCE_ENABLED` is off), and the `CONVERGENCE ADVISORY NOTES` block of `scripts/review_rb_judge.sh` (read from the ledger, or when `REVIEW_LEDGER_ENABLED` is off from this run's `review_convergence_advisory.txt`, falling back to the `=== ADVISORY ISSUE` stubs in `review_issues.txt` on a same-head resume). A missing bundle or floor file, or a gawk error, applies nothing; an error logs `outcome=continue reason=error`. `REVIEW_CONVERGENCE_ENABLED=false` skips everything.
