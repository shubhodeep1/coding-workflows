- **Review convergence rule (issue #10056).** `review_convergence_classify` in `scripts/review_issue_ledger.sh` runs after the reject verifier and before the ledger (also when `REVIEW_LEDGER_ENABLED=0`). From round `REVIEW_CONVERGENCE_FROM_ROUND` (default `3`, valid `2`-`5`; round = `REVIEW_CONVERGENCE_AUTOFIX_COUNT` from the `retrigger_guard` step's `autofix_iteration` plus one, falling back to `AUTOFIX_ITERATION`) and with at least `REVIEW_CONVERGENCE_MIN_REVIEWERS + 1` non-empty reviewer sections in `reviewer_bundle.txt`, an `=== ISSUE` block classified `must-fix`, `nice-to-have`, `unclassified` or empty is reclassified `advisory-lone` (with `CONVERGENCE_PRIOR_CLASSIFICATION`, `CONVERGENCE_REVIEWERS`, `CONVERGENCE_ROUND` lines) when fewer than `REVIEW_CONVERGENCE_MIN_REVIEWERS` (default `2`) reviewer sections support it, it has no `FLOOR_CRITICAL_KEYWORD:*` floor row on the same file within +/-3 lines, and its `LENS` is neither `SECURITY & INPUT VALIDATION` nor `IMPLICIT-EXECUTION & TRUST-BOUNDARY RISKS`. `FLOOR_HIGH_CONFIDENCE` and `FLOOR_MULTI_REVIEWER` never exempt (AD-1). A section supports a finding with a `path:line` / `FILE:`+`LINE:` anchor on the same file within +/-3 lines of `LINES`, or by containing an `EVIDENCE` quote of at least 24 characters; only `FLAGGED_BY`-named sections count, or every supporting section when no name matches. Section content is bounded by the bundle's `BYTES:` line, so reviewer text cannot forge a section. A model-written `advisory-lone` is always re-evaluated. The step writes `${RUNTIME_DIR}/review_convergence_advisory.txt` (TSV: block id, `file:lines`, lens, severity, reviewers, round, sanitised summary of at most 200 bytes); after the residual rewrite `review_convergence_stub_advisory_blocks` turns those blocks into `=== ADVISORY ISSUE <id> ===` stubs (a RESIDUAL stub wins). Ledger entries gain optional `CONVERGENCE: advisory-lone`, `CONVERGENCE_ROUND`, `CONVERGENCE_REVIEWERS` lines that `parse_prior_ledger` accepts. Consumers: the editor prompt in `scripts/review_apply_fixes.sh` (block + directive only when the TSV is non-empty), the "Not blocking (one reviewer)" section of `scripts/review_autofix_step_post_editor_summary.sh`, and the `CONVERGENCE ADVISORY NOTES` block of `scripts/review_rb_judge.sh`. A missing bundle or floor file, or a gawk error, applies nothing. `REVIEW_CONVERGENCE_ENABLED=false` skips everything.
