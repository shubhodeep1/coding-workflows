The `promote-candidate` branch (Phase 2 of `docs/plans/pin-promote-cycle-to-candidate-plan.md`): the 13 `.github/workflows/internal-*.yml` wrappers call their reusable workflows at `@promote-candidate`, and `.github/workflows/promote-candidate-follow.yml` (push to `main`, hourly at `:17`, dispatch; `GH_PAT`, `main` only) runs `scripts/promote_candidate_follow.sh`, which fast-forwards the branch to the `main` tip with `force=false` (creating it when missing) unless the `promote-cycle-frozen` tag freezes it, deletes a freeze tag older than `PROMOTE_CYCLE_FREEZE_MAX_SECS` (default `172800`) with a CRITICAL alert, ignores the tag with `PROMOTE_CYCLE_PIN_ENABLED=false` (default `true`), and sends one CRITICAL alert per UTC day while the branch is unprotected (the owner ruleset is an operator step); the Claude pool broker accepts `refs/heads/promote-candidate` only for this repository and only when the signed `job_workflow_sha` is an ancestor of `main` or `stable`.
