- **Release runner-wait check measures job wait (issue #10181).** `release_gate_oldest_queued_wait` in `scripts/gh_helpers.sh` answers for the `runner_wait_long` skip in `scripts/auto_release_stable.sh` and `scripts/promote_main_cycle.sh` (cycle and poller retry check). It lists live queued runs created between the wedged cutoff and `now - RELEASE_GATE_MAX_RUNNER_WAIT_MINUTES` (call 1, plus call 2 for the last page past 100 runs), then reads the jobs of at most 3 of those runs, oldest `created_at` first (`GET repos/<repo>/actions/runs/<id>/jobs?per_page=100&filter=latest`, no read when there is no candidate). A run with no jobs is held by a `concurrency:` group and a run with no `queued` job is not waiting; both are ignored. `outcome=long` only when the oldest `queued` job across the runs read has waited the limit or longer; `oldest_queued_min` is that job's wait (also on `short`). Output: `outcome=<long|short|read_failed> oldest_queued_min=<n|-> queued_in_window=<n|-> calls=<0..5>[ runs_unread=<n>]`; `runs_unread` appears only on `short` when more than 3 candidates existed, so the log shows the check was bounded; past 100 listed runs it also counts the runs on the pages not fetched (`total_count` minus the last page's runs). A failed or unparseable jobs read (including a queued job whose `created_at` does not parse) prints `outcome=read_failed` and the callers proceed (fail open). Callers parse `calls=` without anchoring at the end of the line.
