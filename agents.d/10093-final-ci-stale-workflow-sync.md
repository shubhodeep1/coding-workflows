**Final CI cut off under a stale workflow file goes to the sweep's base sync (issue #10093, Refs #9895).**
- A final CI run is dispatched on the PR head ref, so it runs the branch's own `ci.yml` and its `timeout-minutes`. `scripts/ci_cancelled_rerun.py classify-run` gains `--stale-workflow-head <40-hex>` and `--stale-workflow-base <ref>`; with both, the lint-aggregate-only path of `classify_runner_shutdown` returns `stale_workflow_timeout` when every job `lint` names is `cancelled` with no step concluded `failure` and a failure annotation matching `The job (running on runner … )?has exceeded the maximum execution time of`, no abandoned job is mixed in, no other job is red, `lint` failed only at "Require every CI job to succeed", and `.github/workflows/ci.yml` differs between the head and the base (two contents GETs; equal blob SHAs, or a base `timeout-minutes` for the job that is not higher than the head's when both parse, give `genuine`). Other annotations are ignored: passing tests print `##[error]` lines. The verdict is not in `INFRA_VERDICTS`, so the sweep's re-run never acts on it. Without the arguments the classifier is unchanged.
- `scripts/review_final_ci_failed.sh` reads the base ref from its existing `GET pulls/<n>` and passes the stale arguments only in `shubhodeep1/coding-workflows`, on attempt 1, for a same-repository head outside `orchestrator/project-*`, with `FINAL_CI_SWEEP_BASE_SYNC_ENABLED` on and `FINAL_CI_SWEEP_BASE_SYNC_MAX` above 0, and with `FINAL_CI_STALE_WORKFLOW_SYNC_ENABLED` (default `true`; `false` off; any other value off with a warning) on. On the verdict it posts `<!-- ai:final-ci-stale-workflow:v1 pr=<n> head=<sha> -->`, no failed marker and no dispatch. A failed marker POST falls back to the hand-back.
- `_pr_base_sync_count <pr> <head sha>` (`scripts/pr_checks_lib.sh`) sets `PR_BASE_SYNC_STALE_WORKFLOW_MARKED` from its existing comments read: `true` for a Bot or OWNER/MEMBER/COLLABORATOR comment carrying that marker for the head when no such comment carries the head's `ai:final-ci-failed:v1` marker. `scripts/review_final_ci_sweep.sh` syncs a marked behind head through the unchanged base-sync path; on a terminal skip (`max_syncs`, `up_to_date`, `freshness_disabled`, `gate_failure|error|absent|untrusted`, `update_branch_refused`, the switch off at sweep time, `stale_workflow_sync_disabled`, or syncing off: `cap_zero` for `FINAL_CI_SWEEP_BASE_SYNC_MAX=0` and `base_sync_disabled` for `FINAL_CI_SWEEP_BASE_SYNC_ENABLED` off, where the pass still reads each failed candidate's comments within the per-tick cap because an older branch's `ci.yml` does not pass those switches to the deferral) it runs the default-branch `review_final_ci_failed.sh` with the stale and infra switches off, which hands the head back. Transient skips retry. The sweep step exports `FINAL_CI_STALE_WORKFLOW_SYNC_ENABLED`, `FINAL_CI_FAILED_REDISPATCH_ENABLED` and `ALLOW_WORKFLOW_EDITS` for that.
- Tests: `tests/test_ci_cancelled_rerun.py`, `tests/test_review_final_ci.py`.

<!-- agents: section="Stable log prefixes (contractual)" -->
- `REVIEW_FINAL_CI_FAILED` also logs `outcome=stale_workflow_sync_deferred reason=stale_workflow_timeout` (`scripts/review_final_ci_failed.sh`, issue #10093): the final run was cut off at a job time limit under the branch's older `ci.yml`; the stale-workflow marker was posted and the sweep's base sync takes over. `REVIEW_FINAL_CI_BASE_SYNC` lines (`scripts/review_final_ci_sweep.sh`) gain the optional trailing key `stale_workflow=handed_back` (or `stale_workflow=dry_run`) when a marked head that cannot be synced was handed back to review, `stale_workflow=hand_back_failed` when the hand-back script did not log `outcome=redispatched` or `outcome=already_reported` (the head retries next tick), and the skip reasons `stale_workflow_sync_disabled` and `base_sync_disabled` (`cap_zero` now comes after the comments read).
