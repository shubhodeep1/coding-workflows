<!-- agents: section="Workflow architecture" -->
Heal intake evidence pack (P1 of `docs/plans/heal-evidence-reproduction-and-trends-plan.md`, #8435): with `WORKFLOW_HEAL_EVIDENCE_PACK_ENABLED=true` (default `false`; the intake defaults it itself, and both the `intake` and `recovery-scan` jobs of `.github/workflows/workflow-failure-heal-intake.yml` pass the repo variable) `scripts/workflow_failure_heal_intake.sh` runs `_heal_build_evidence_pack` right after `_heal_collect_linked_runs`, so after `scope_inputs_frozen.json` is written read-only; the pack never feeds the `ai:workflow-heal-scope:v1` marker, the fingerprint or the diagnosis prompt. `workflow_failure_heal.py evidence-pack` renders an ordered list of subsections (later evidence is appended to that list): the step timeline of each reported run (`render_failed_run_timeline`, from `${LOG_DIR}/run-<id>-info.json` and `run-<id>-jobs.json`) and each linked run's timeline (`render_linked_run_timeline(include_head_refs=True)`, from `${RUNTIME_DIR}/linked_runs/`), both with the `[LONG]` marks and the 8 KiB / 20-job / 40-step caps of the linked-run renderer plus a second header line `head_sha: <12> | head_branch: <branch>`. With its defaults `render_linked_run_timeline` output is unchanged. Each subsection goes through `redact_secrets`, `sanitize_text`, `neutralize_untrusted_routing` (`<!--` becomes `&lt;!--`, routing keys gain `(untrusted)`) and the fence escape, sits in a `text` fence under `## Evidence pack` and an untrusted-data banner, and the whole pack is capped at `WORKFLOW_HEAL_EVIDENCE_PACK_MAX_BYTES` (default 12000, clamped to 1024-20000; the first subsection that does not fit is cut at a line with its fence closed). `compose-issue --evidence-pack-file` inserts it before the diagnosis, neutralizes it again and then runs the untrusted-body check and `validate_heal_issue_body_routing` over the whole body; the flag is passed only when the switch is on and the pack has text, so a switch-off body is byte-identical. No GitHub API call is added.
