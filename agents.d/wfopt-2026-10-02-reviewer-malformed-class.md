- `REVIEWER_ATTEMPT_RESULT` field extension (`scripts/review_run_reviewers.sh`, `reviewer_attempt_result_failure_class` / `reviewer_attempt_result_line`; extends the existing `REVIEWER_ATTEMPT_RESULT` entry, prefix already registered). `failure_class` adds `malformed_structured_output`: the attempt stderr ends a line with `OpenCode structured reviewer output was malformed.` and no `max_steps`, `stall_guard`, `timeout`, `tool_repeat`, `rate_limit`, `server_error` or `provider_request_invalid` cause matched first. `empty_structured_output` (the no-text marker) is unchanged and is checked before the malformed marker. The line gains a trailing `output_bytes=<int>` field after `failure_class`: the byte count of the raw OpenCode structured output file, measured before ANSI stripping or deletion; `0` for pre-launch failures and for any missing, unreadable or non-integer value. No output text is ever printed. Log only: retry and failback decisions do not read either field.
