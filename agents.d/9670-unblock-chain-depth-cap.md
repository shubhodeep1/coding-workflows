**Unblock judge chain depth cap (Refs #9670).** `scripts/unblock_ledger.py` `decide()` stops the judge from splitting an item that is already two re-issues, follow-ups or fix-ups away from the original (`MAX_CHAIN_DEPTH = 2`; chain #9190 -> #9261 -> PR #9283 -> #9398 / #9670). `chain_depth(title)` counts the chain prefixes a title starts with: `Re-issue of #N: `, `Follow-up to #N: `, `Unblock descope for #N: `, `Unblock operator step for #N: `, `Unblock fix-up for #N: `. `scripts/unblock_judge.sh` passes the title as `decide --chain-title`: the item's own title, or for a trusted PR whose head branch is `ai/issue-<n>` the title of issue `<n>` (context field `chain_title`; one GET per judged trusted PR, §15: `item.json` is the PR and no earlier call returns that issue; a failed read falls back to the PR title). At the cap `CHAIN_VERDICTS` (`reissue`, `accept_with_followup`, `descope`, `operator_step`) are removed from `allowed`, the decision carries `chain_depth` and `chain_capped`, and `validate` refuses such a verdict like any other that is not on the menu. The judge can then only continue on the item itself; when no verdict is left the item is terminal with `menu_exhausted` and takes the existing path (the needs-human park, or the legacy close with the digest off), so the scan treats it as parked. In `scripts/unblock_actions.py`, when `chain_title` has chain prefixes the new issue's title is built from it, so the lineage survives the PR hop; otherwise titles are composed as before. The conflict resolver's host-only re-issue does not go through `decide` and is not capped. Titles are editable: the cap is a brake on the pipeline's own chains, not a trust boundary.
