`scripts/review_head_gate.sh freshness-sync` also reviews an update head normally when the full final CI failed on it: a comment for that head ending in `<!-- ai:final-ci-failed:v1 pr=<n> head=<sha> -->` (`scripts/review_final_ci_failed.sh`, `ci.yml`'s `final-ci-failed` job) or `<!-- ai:final-ci-repair-request:v1 pr=<n> head=<sha> -->` (the review-blocked judge, issue #10063), posted by `github-actions[bot]` (type `Bot`) or the `GH_PAT` login, gives `REVIEW_HEAD_GATE mode=freshness_sync ... outcome=review reason=final_ci_failed_on_head`. It reads the gate's existing marker-comments file, so it adds no API call. Before this only `ai:freshness-ci-failed:v1` was read there: PRs #10152, #10133, #10070 and #9902 were skipped and re-approved on every review run on 2026-11-28 while `ci/final` stayed red.
