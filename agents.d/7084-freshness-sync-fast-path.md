- **Freshness updates after approval (#7084).** `_pr_base_fresh_for_merge` posts `<!-- ai:merge-base-sync:v1 pr=<n> from=<approved head> sync=<k> -->` after each accepted `update-branch` (`_pr_base_sync_marker_post`, log `MERGE_BASE_SYNC_MARKER`). It stops updating once the workflow login's markers on the PR reach `MERGE_BASE_FRESHNESS_MAX_SYNCS` (default 2; `_pr_base_sync_count`), and then returns 0 so the caller merges after its required-checks wait (`reason=max_syncs`). In the review gate, `gate_freshness_sync_fast_path` runs `scripts/review_head_gate.sh freshness-sync` on the marker comments the gate already read. It skips the reviewer panel (`DET_SKIP_REASON=freshness_sync`, routed to `deterministic-skip-merge`) only when the head is GitHub's own merge of the marker's `from` head with a commit already on the base: two parents, `from` first, committer `web-flow` with a verified signature, and `compare/<second parent>...<base>` `ahead` or `identical`. Any failed check or read reviews normally. `force-review` wins, and `MERGE_BASE_SYNC_FAST_PATH_ENABLED=false` turns the fast path off.
