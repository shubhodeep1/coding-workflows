- **Several merge-lease holders per base (merge-lease parallel merges Phase 5, Refs #9781).** `MERGE_LEASE_MAX_HOLDERS` (default `1`, range 1-5; `_merge_lease_max_holders` in `scripts/pr_checks_lib.sh` reads `${MERGE_LEASE_MAX_HOLDERS:-1}`, a value that is not a whole number of at most three digits gives 1 and one sanitized `::warning::` on stderr, 0 gives 1, above 5 gives 5) is wired as a repository variable next to every `MERGE_LEASE_SOFT_PATHS` line of `review_autofix.yml` (3), `orchestrate_poll.yml` (4) and `cancel_on_pr_close.yml` (1), and after `MERGE_LEASE_WAIT_LABEL` in `review_autofix_sweep.yml`.
  - Record (`refs/merge-lease/<base>`, still `merge-lease v1`): one holder is written in the old shape, byte for byte. With 2+ live holders `pr=`/`head=`/`acquired=`/`updated=` describe the primary, `expires=` is the latest holder expiry (an old reader sees the primary plus the union of paths, a stricter lease), then the `skip=` lines, one `holder=<pr> head=<sha|> expires=<epoch> acquired=<epoch> updated=<epoch>` line per holder (primary first) and `holder_path=<pr> <path>` lines, then `paths:` with the union (`*` when a holder is `*` or the union is over `MERGE_LEASE_MAX_PATHS`). A holder path holding CR or starting with `*` is written as `*` (`_merge_lease_paths_record_form`).
  - `_merge_lease_read` also sets `MERGE_LEASE_HOLDER_ROWS` (live rows `pr head expires acquired updated`, head `-` when unknown), `MERGE_LEASE_HOLDER_ALL_ROWS` (expired rows too), `MERGE_LEASE_HOLDER_PATHS` (`pr<TAB>path`) and `MERGE_LEASE_HOLDER_COUNT`. The scalar `MERGE_LEASE_*` fields describe the primary from its own `holder=` line; an expired primary is replaced in memory by the oldest-acquired live holder (then lowest PR). `_merge_lease_is_held` is also true while any holder is live. Malformed `holder=` lines are dropped.
  - Writes: `_merge_lease_cas_commit <base> <message>` is the compare-and-swap extracted unchanged from `_merge_lease_write` (0 written, 3 lost race, 1 error); `_merge_lease_write_holders <base> <rows>` writes a holder list through it; `_merge_lease_skip_block`, `_merge_lease_holder_paths`, `_merge_lease_holder_paths_set`, `_merge_lease_holder_row` and `_merge_lease_rows_without` (a removed primary re-orders the rest oldest acquired first) are the helpers.
  - `_merge_lease_gate` calls `_merge_lease_gate_multi` when a holder is live and the cap is above 1 or 2+ holders are live; otherwise the single-holder code runs unchanged. Merge mode waits on the first holder sharing a path (`_merge_lease_paths_intersect` with both heads). A PR that is itself a holder and whose files now share a path with an earlier holder (record order) waits `holder_shares_paths` without the wait label: the tick never grants a live holder and the sweep skips labelled PRs, so the review sweep retries it; every other wait is labelled. Update mode renews the caller's own row, waits `held` (blocker = primary) at the cap, waits `holder_shares_paths` on an overlapping holder, waits `queued_behind` on the first waiter ahead that is disjoint from every holder (overlapping waiters are passed over through `_merge_lease_queue_first`'s exclude list, at most 10 files reads), else adds the caller. A lost race returns 3 and the gate re-decides within its two attempts. `MERGE_LEASE_BLOCKER` stays one PR number.
  - `scripts/review_merge_train.sh`: `_mt_merge_lease_tick` hands a base to `_mt_merge_lease_tick_multi` only when `declare -F` finds `_merge_lease_max_holders`, `_merge_lease_write_holders` and `_merge_lease_rows_without` (`tick_p5_lib`) and the cap is above 1 or 2+ holders are live. It releases each holder with its own checks and its own compare-and-swap write, then grants while below the cap; an overlapping waiter keeps its wait label.
  - Log additions only: `_merge_lease_log` appends ` holders=<n>` with 2+ live holders; the multi tick's `held` and `granted` lines end with `holders=<n>`, and it logs `MERGE_LEASE pr=<n> base=<ref> mode=tick outcome=skipped reason=holder_shares_paths blocker=<holder>` (metric `event=skipped reason=holder_shares_paths holder=<holder>`).
