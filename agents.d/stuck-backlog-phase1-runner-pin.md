- **Runner image pin and nightly canary (stuck-backlog plan Phase 1, Refs #9421).** Hosted jobs in `.github/workflows/` and `workflow-templates/` use `runs-on: ubuntu-26.04`, never the floating `ubuntu-latest` (the 2026-11-10 image move broke `scripts/render_prompt.py`, #9200). `ci.yml` jobs use `${{ inputs.runner_image || 'ubuntu-26.04' }}`, and the three existing `ubuntu-24.04` jobs (`drift-audit.yml`, `security-audit.yml`, `review_autofix_sweep.yml`) stay as they are. The `runner-image-canary` job in `nightly-validation-selftest.yml` calls `ci.yml` (its `workflow_call` trigger) with `runner_image: ubuntu-latest`, forwards no secrets, and alerts through the existing Nightly route in `workflow-failure-heal-intake.yml` (a failed Nightly run becomes one heal issue). `RUNNER_IMAGE_CANARY_ENABLED=false` turns it off; unset means on. Its `permissions` must equal the union of what the `ci.yml` jobs request (today `contents: read, actions: read, issues: write`, from `budget-watch`), because GitHub rejects the whole Nightly run at startup when a nested job asks for more than the caller grants. Jobs in `ci.yml` that write or read a secret must stay `github.event_name == 'push'`-only, and `ci.yml` must not gain a concurrency group without `github.event_name` in it. `.github/actionlint.yaml` declares `ubuntu-26.04` because actionlint 1.7.12 does not know it. To move the pin once the canary is green on a new image, update every `runs-on`, the `ci.yml` fallback, `PINNED_RUNNER_LABEL` in `tests/test_runner_image_pin_contract.py` and `.github/actionlint.yaml` together; that test enforces all of this.
