- `STAGE_SWITCH_WATCH` (`scripts/stage_switch_watch.sh`, `workflow-log-analysis.yml` job `collect-logs`, #9994): `outcome=ok off=<n> waiting=<n> on_purpose=<n> alerts=<n> alert=sent|not_delivered|already_sent_today|telegram_unconfigured|none`, or `outcome=disabled`, `outcome=read_failed reason=no_token|workdir|gh_api|parse|no_snapshot|evaluate`, `outcome=registry_invalid`, and `outcome=snapshot_ready` after the read step. Never fails the job.
LOG_PREFIX.name=STAGE_SWITCH_WATCH
- `STAGE_SWITCH_OFF` (`scripts/stage_switch_watch.py evaluate`, #9994): one line per registry switch whose value is neither `true` nor one of the entry's `on_values`: `name=<VAR> value=<true|false|0|1|yes|no|on|off|<non-boolean>|<redacted>> default=true off_since=<ISO|unknown> hours_off=<h|unknown> status=waiting|off_on_purpose|alert marker=none|valid|expired`. Raw values are never logged.
LOG_PREFIX.name=STAGE_SWITCH_OFF

<!-- agents: section="Workflow architecture" -->
- **Stage switch watch (#9994).** `workflow-log-analysis.yml` `collect-logs` ends with four `continue-on-error` steps: read (one paginated `actions/variables` listing with `GH_PAT` into a sanitized snapshot in `RUNNER_TEMP`), a lookup-only `actions/cache/restore` of `stage-switch-off-alert-<UTC day>`, report (Telegram secrets only; `scripts/stage_switch_watch.sh report`), and `actions/cache/save` of the day marker after a delivered alert. The read step runs even when the watch is off, so `outcome=disabled` is logged. The switches are discovered at run time (see "Stage switch discovery (#10170)"); the registry `scripts/stage_switches.json` (`stage_switches.v1`: `switches[{name, default?, stage?, on_values?, pending?}]`, `off_on_purpose[{name, until, reason, pending?}]`) holds only detail that cannot be derived; `on_values` (subset of `1`, `yes`, `on`) is set only when every consumer keeps the stage on for those values. Knobs: `STAGE_SWITCH_WATCH_ENABLED` (default `true`), `STAGE_SWITCH_OFF_ALERT_HOURS` (default `24`).
