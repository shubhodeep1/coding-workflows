- **Failing-step log anchor (issue #8799).** `scripts/collect_pr_check_runs_context.py` `_fetch_log_tail` keeps its three requests per failed check run (job-logs redirect, `HEAD`, one ranged `GET`) but scans up to `LOG_SCAN_BYTES` (256 KiB) from the end of the log. `_select_log_tail_window` cuts every line over 2,000 characters (`[… N chars truncated]`) and, when the scan holds a `##[error]` line (never the partial first line of a ranged scan), returns the lines ending at the first one plus up to 5 after it, trimmed from the front to `CHECK_RUNS_LOG_TAIL_BYTES` bytes and `LOG_TAIL_LINES` lines; otherwise it returns the pre-#8799 tail, except that when a line in that tail is over 2,000 characters, every scanned line is cut first and whole lines are kept from the end within the same byte and line budget. Before any cut, the collector masks credential shapes on the full decoded scan with `_redact_log_text` (a local copy of the simple `workflow_failure_heal._SECRET_PATTERNS`, since the helper is staged alone), and the partial first line of a ranged scan is never kept as lead context. In the triage `test_failure` tier, lead lines are dropped until the matched line fits `MAX_EXCERPT_BYTES`. `failed[i].log_tail` starts with `log_tail_anchor: step_error|end_of_log` (informational, outside the byte cap). In `scripts/check_triage_resolution.py`, `fetch_failing_tracebacks` runs `heal.redact_secrets` on the whole raw log, then the local `_cap_long_lines` (same 2,000-character rule; a local copy because clarify stages only listed helpers), then the unchanged `heal.filter_log`. `_excerpt_with_anchor` picks the first matching tier: `test_failure` (`TEST_FAILURE_RE`, from 10 lines before), `step_error` (the `MAX_EXCERPT_LINES` lines ending at the first `##[error]` plus up to 5 after, trimmed from the front so the error line survives `MAX_EXCERPT_BYTES`), `traceback`, then `legacy` (`TRACEBACK_START_RE`, byte-identical to the old `_excerpt`). The traceback comment adds an `Excerpt anchor` bullet naming the tier, for tiers in `EXCERPT_ANCHORS` only; the `ai:check-triage-traceback:v1` marker is unchanged.
