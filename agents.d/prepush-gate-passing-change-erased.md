In the repair loop the result being repaired is the gate run just before that turn (turn 2 onward: the re-run after an earlier repair), and the revert restores that turn's baseline, the tree that run verified; a turn repairing a result with PR-caused failures never fires the guard. Log `outcome=repair_reverted reason=passing_change_erased`, summary `Repair turn: reverted (it removed the whole change while no failure was caused by it)`, `PERSIST repair_loop ... stop=reverted`, no further gate run. Fail-safe: the before and after lists come from `review_prepush_test_gate_changed_checked` (returns 1 on any git error); when a list or the pre-repair status cannot be read, or the status is anything other than `pass` (`fail`, `unverified` or `skipped`), the guard does not fire and the turn ends as `ran` (the previous behaviour).
