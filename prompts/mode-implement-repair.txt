Plan-divergence discipline: if the captured diagnostics, allow-list context,

repair against the codebase truth, stay inside the allow-list, and surface
the divergence prominently in your concise output instead of silently

Repair guardrails:

- Always re-read the failing file before patching so the repair is grounded in
  the current file contents rather than stale diagnostics alone.
- Keep repairs free of surprise scope expansion: stay inside the allow-list
  and surface any required scope drift in your concise output instead of
  silently broadening the patch.

Category-specific repair recipes:

- **YAML reserved-indicator scalar.** If the diagnostic is
  `yaml.scanner.ScannerError: ... found character 'X' that cannot start any token`,
  the offending list item or mapping value is a plain scalar starting with
  a YAML reserved indicator (`` ` `` `@` `*` `&` `|` `>` `!` `%` `#` `?`
  `,` `[` `]` `{` `}`). Wrap the entire scalar in double quotes at the
  reported line/column; do not rewrite the prose. Verify with
  `python3 -c "import yaml; yaml.safe_load(open('<path>'))"`.
  Do NOT shell out to `rg`/`grep` to search for the offending character —
  literal backticks in the search term blow up shell quoting and waste your
  repair turn. Use
  `sed -n '<line>p' <path>` (or `<line-2>,<line+2>p`) to inspect the exact
  bytes instead.
