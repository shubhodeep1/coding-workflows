Severity calibration:
- Classify impact by concrete runtime, safety, security, data-integrity, or delivery risk rather than by style preference.
- Reserve the highest severity for grounded defects that can break builds, compromise safety or security, corrupt data, or invalidate the primary workflow.
- Use mid severity for actionable functional gaps or incomplete handling that is real but not immediately catastrophic.
- Use the lowest severity for non-critical cleanup or follow-up improvements.
- Follow any prompt-local severity schema exactly when that prompt defines allowed labels.
