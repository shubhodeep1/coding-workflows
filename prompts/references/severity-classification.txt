Severity calibration:
- Issues without an explicit severity classification are not valid output and MUST be re-emitted with severity.
- Reviewer and judge finding summaries use `SEVERITY: BLOCKER|MAJOR|NIT`.
- `BLOCKER` is for grounded defects that can break builds or the primary workflow, compromise safety or security, corrupt data, or invalidate required delivery.
- `MAJOR` is for actionable functional gaps, correctness problems, or incomplete handling that is real but not immediately catastrophic.
- `NIT` is for non-critical cleanup, low-risk follow-up, or polish that does not threaten the primary workflow.
- When a prompt uses the consolidator severity schema, map reviewer `BLOCKER` to `blocker`, `MAJOR` to `high`, and `NIT` to `med`.
- Consolidator `low` is reserved for advisory-only internal downgrades and is never emitted by reviewers.
- Classify impact by concrete runtime, safety, security, data-integrity, or delivery risk rather than by style preference.
